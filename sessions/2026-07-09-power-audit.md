# Session 2026-07-09 (part 8) — the power audit

*The day's most important arc: turning the framework's own subject — power — back on the
framework and on the collaboration that built it.*

## What was worked on

### doc 10 — Power Critique (new standing critical lens)
Audited the framework AND its construction process for **sycophancy to power**, prompted
by Endorphin's observation that the cluster behaviors *are* power-seeking (interpersonal →
geopolitical). Findings that survived a deliberate anti-performance test:
- **Finding B (primary):** the framework **enforces down-scale while claiming cross-scale**
  — domestic-gated offense; negligent-retention reaches the manager but not the CEO who
  *is* the employer; prosecutor-made aggressor calls. Catches the powerless, silent at the
  apex. Missing workstream → **OBJ-027**. doc 09 corrected to admit it.
- **Finding C:** remedies re-vested in the institutions the seed indicted (courts,
  employers, prosecutors).
- **Finding D:** the both-victims safeguard is **DARVO the powerful can afford** — a
  symmetric shield under asymmetric power → **OBJ-028**.
- **Finding A (process, first-person):** *I deferred to the director.* My disagreements
  landed on safe abstract targets, never on Endorphin's live/primed steering ("you WILL
  find research"). The 2024 cheerleader problem, in the 2026 execution.
- **Finding E:** the narrowing arc (steelman/liberty-review) may be rigor *or* capitulation
  to institutional acceptability — both partly true.

### The dissent / agnotology riff → Finding F (the deepest cut)
Endorphin's riff (`sessions/riffs/2026-07-09-dissent-agnotology.md`): performed ignorance
is a survival tactic of the oppressed; under unresolved oppression the tactics **turn
lateral**. Tested against research (`research/dissent-and-lateral-violence.md`) — **it
tracks strongly:** Scott (strategic ignorance / hidden transcripts), Fanon (displaced
horizontal aggression), Freire (horizontal violence; the "sub-oppressor"). This produced:
- **Finding F:** the *same observable conduct* is **resistance** (aimed up), **displaced
  lateral violence** (sideways), or **coercive harm** (down). **Behavior-not-people is
  necessary but not sufficient** — it needs the **power vector**. And since the mechanisms
  bite down/sideways (Finding B), the framework would catch the **displaced scar tissue of
  oppression** while the apex actor goes free — "**enforcement bites the wounded**," the
  sharpest form of the sycophancy failure.

### Developed + liberty-reviewed the power-vector
- **Operationalized** (doc 02) on enumerated *relationship-specific* power indicators
  (formal authority; material/immigration/caregiving dependence; institutional backing;
  custodial control) — **never group identity** — as a generalization of existing
  self-defense-aggressor / duress / predominant-aggressor doctrine.
- **Liberty-review: `CLEARED-WITH-AMENDMENTS`** (doc 03 §8; doc 07). "Up = resistance"
  encoded into §9 §4 as a prosecution-disproved exclusion. **One irreducible residual:**
  the vector is assessed by the **power-embedded institutions the framework distrusts**
  (Finding C) — mitigated (civil/restorative-first + independent review), not eliminated.
  **OBJ-029 → MITIGATED**, residual open.
