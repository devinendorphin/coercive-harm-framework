### develop-04 — foreseeability reframing (doc 04 → ACTIVE)
Research (`research/foreseeability-and-risk-assessment.md`):
- **s.76 already contains the foreseeability limb.** "Knows or **ought to know**" =
  an objective reasonable-person test, already on the books. The reframing *re-weights*
  onto it; it does not invent it.
- **The pattern's harm is professionally predictable.** Validated actuarial
  instruments predict serious harm up to homicide from documented coercive-control
  patterns: Danger Assessment (Campbell), SARA, ODARA, DASH, MOSAIC-20 (Graham et al.
  2021 review). Endorphin's "if the cluster is predictable, foreseeability is easy" —
  confirmed.
- **"Something Was Wrong"** = qualitative pattern-convergence (many independent
  survivors, same tactics → same effects) + jury-comprehensible texture; flagged
  *illustrative, not a dataset* (selection bias).
- **Structural payoff:** rebuild the offense as **endangerment** (foreseeably-harmful
  conduct is the crime; actual harm = sentencing aggravator).

### /liberty-review — endangerment reframing (doc 03 §8, doc 07)
`CLEARED-WITH-AMENDMENTS` as **qualified endangerment**. The pivotal finding:
- It does **not escape** the serious-effect element — prongs 3 (weaponization) and 5
  (speech) both **require retaining a foreseeable-harm nexus.** So the offense goes
  from "prove *this victim's actual harm*" → "prove the *conduct was objectively
  harm-capable*." That removes re-traumatization, resilience-penalty, and
  individual-causation, while keeping the element that anchors speech and blocks
  weaponization. **The foreseeability nexus is the new keystone.**
- Amendments: enumerated conduct + course-of-conduct + objective foreseeability +
  predominant-aggressor safeguard + protective-response exclusion + speech-anchoring
  in non-speech conduct + *Apprendi* discipline on the harm aggravator + civil-first.
