## The rubric, as stated (Endorphin's, not reworded into a checklist)

- **Fluent smear:** speaking about an "abusive ex" fluently, calling her crazy, putting her image "through the
  muck to people who don't even know her." That points toward the speaker as the perpetrator.
- **Survivor still inside it:** endless justifying of the partner, resistance to honest talk, shutting the
  friend down.
- **Survivor first realising:** an incoherent first telling, "through tears… pre-linguistic yells… through the
  body. The mouth is secondary." "That's the trauma trying to find words."
- **Lingo without the felt knowledge:** abuse terms used with no trauma-informed sense behind them. Asked what
  kind of abuse, the speaker keeps it to the physical and does not know the emotional-abuse shorthand already
  in the culture.
- **Arms race:** each sign gets learned and counterfeited.
