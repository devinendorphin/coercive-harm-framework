## Routing

- **Opportunity, not variance** → series outline arc 1; doc 02 (taxonomy) as a refinement of C1:
  *low variance in conduct, conditional on opportunity.* `[HYPOTHESIS]` until tested. Test:
  code opportunity per case (shared home, control of money, institutional authority, duration,
  children, access) from facts independent of the tactics, before coding the tactics; prediction:
  where opportunity exists, the moves appear. **Refuted by** cases with high opportunity and the
  move absent, or moves that do not track opportunity. Without independent coding of
  opportunity, "they didn't have the ability" explains any absence after the fact.
- **Survivor shorthand** → evidence for convergent *conduct* (C1), which survivors recognise from
  behaviour. Watch-out: shared vocabulary (frame words run 11.8× the control), though R4 found
  convergence survives in the low-vocabulary half.
- **Early developmental difference / prefrontal** → standing note **S-1** (mPFC idea: illustrative,
  never load-bearing; OBJ-002) and **S-8** (sort on documented conduct, never status). `[HYPOTHESIS]`;
  "second only to gender" `[NORMATIVE]`/untestable as phrased. Evidence both ways: reduced
  vmPFC–amygdala connectivity in psychopathic inmates (Motzkin et al. 2011, *J Neurosci*);
  early adversity and attachment problems as risk factors; against a single difference,
  perpetrator typologies (Holtzworth-Munroe & Stuart 1994: three subtypes) and findings that
  perpetrators fit no single personality or diagnostic category. Kept out of every mechanism and
  out of the public series except as a labelled hypothesis.
