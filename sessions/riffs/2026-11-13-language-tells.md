## Claims checked against general knowledge (S-18, one test)

- "Language… maybe 5,000 years": **that is the age of writing** (about 3200 BCE). Spoken language is far older;
  estimates are contested but run from tens of thousands to a few hundred thousand years.
- "30,000 years for a tool to change the body": the source is not identified. Bodies can respond faster than
  that (lactase persistence spread within roughly the last 10,000 years) and also much slower (cooking and
  jaw/gut change, over hundreds of thousands to millions of years). Recorded as Endorphin's framing, not as a
  figure.
