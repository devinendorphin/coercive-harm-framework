## Core claim

Performed ignorance ("I don't know nothin'") is a survival tactic developed by the
oppressed to avoid the punishment of the master/slaver/policeman — effective. But when
oppression persists **unresolved** over enough time, the same tactics **turn lateral** —
deployed against peers in the same stratum — becoming the cluster behaviors the framework
describes. "See if that tracks in research."

## Verdict: it tracks (see `research/dissent-and-lateral-violence.md`)

Strongly, via Fanon and Freire (the lateral-conversion) and Scott (the strategic
ignorance). Three honest corrections: (1) "agnotology" (Proctor) is usually ignorance
manufactured *by* the powerful — the subaltern version is closer to Scott/Mills; (2) the
mechanism is framed as *structural* (can't hit up → hit sideways) more than *temporal*;
(3) the frame *explains without excusing* — the peer being harmed laterally is still
harmed. Routed to doc 10 (power critique) and doc 02 (taxonomy: the power-vector problem).
