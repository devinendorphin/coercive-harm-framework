# Told Whole — series outline (draft 2026-11-12, arcs not episodes)

**Anchor:** low variance in conduct: the same moves under different names, in every room, **as far
as the room allows** (Endorphin, 2026-11-12: variation in what a perpetrator manages to do is
largely variation in opportunity, not in the perpetrator). Every arc also shows where the pattern
breaks, so the series does not overclaim its anchor.

1. **Same moves, as far as the room allows (2–3 reels).** One timeline shape, relabelled per
   perpetrator; where a move is missing, ask whether the room allowed it. Breaks: isolation not
   elevated in most seasons by the pre-registered test (which did not account for opportunity);
   72 recorded divergences. Opportunity is a hypothesis until coded independently.
2. **Every room (2–3 reels).** Marriage, family, Jonestown, church, workplace, military, the
   troubled-teen industry, a university, a birth centre. Breaks: no setting difference detectable
   either way (weak evidence).
3. **How the moves travel (1–2 reels).** *(Added 2026-11-12.)* Same moves without anyone coordinating:
   actors pick up what others left behind (stigmergy). US consent law as the worked case: a family tree
   from New York 1965 and Michigan 1974 across 49 of 51 jurisdictions; one route through a 1976 model
   statute. Old burdens surviving by inheritance: New York's "overcomes earnest resistance" (1965, deleted
   1982) lives on as Washington's "overcomes resistance"; the Model Penal Code's "woman of ordinary
   resolution" in Ohio, Pennsylvania, Wyoming. And coming back: Nebraska's 1995 reform recognised "no" in
   words or conduct and wrote a resistance duty into statute in the same act. Breaks: documented
   instances, not a measured rate; no intent shown, which is the point; laws copying laws is ordinary
   policy diffusion, and in Title IX policies the shared wording was mostly the federal rule itself
   (mandate, not stigmergy). Source: veriticide ledger, stigmergy test files (2026-11-12).
4. **The room around them (3–4 reels).** Moments vs whole stories (63 % vs 1 in 5 protected);
   disbelief and blame split, one in two for central survivors.
5. **Who did the work (1–2 reels).** Self-rescue, investigators, pooling; the medical-rescue ruling.
6. **Never heard (1–2 reels).** About 3 in 10 with no institutional contact; reasons; others mentioned.
7. **How measuring can hide it (2 reels).** Shortcuts that copied the silencing; "unclear" hiding the
   unheard; the blinding breach; a one-sentence framing cue.
8. **At scale (1–2 reels).** "Fascism is interpersonal violence at scale" as mechanism-homology
   and forward-only continuity (S-6; Biderman, Herman); not tested by this corpus; power position
   did not predict impunity here.
9. **What would help (2 reels).** Delay as extraction; expedite the process, never the proof; civil
   endangerment orders; untested records both ways.

Not a claim of the series: any cause beneath the conduct (S-1). Survivors' shorthand is shown as
recognition of conduct.
