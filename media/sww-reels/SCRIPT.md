## 08 · The Correction · Before S7  (67 s)

- Six seasons in, my method got corrected. The correction was right.
  - *on screen:* The correction. — codebook amendment 1
- I had been scoring discard as absent whenever the target left first. Sarah walked out in season one, and I counted it as a miss for the pattern.
  - *on screen:* cycle ring: my original coding [yyyyny]

  - *on screen:* Her exit is not his behaviour. — Discard is now “not observable” when she leaves first.

  - *on screen:* Leaving is the dangerous time. — Outcome variance is expected, not a counter-case.
- So the codebook split in three. Perpetrator behaviour, the only fields that test the claim. Target responses. And outcomes.
  - *on screen:* Three kinds of field: Perpetrator behaviour · the test; Target responses · recorded; Outcomes · recorded

  - *on screen:* New field: response to separation. — Hoover · reversal · smear · legal abuse · stalking · threats
- One more. Isolation and smears outlast contact. Someone who leaves and faces nothing new has still lost the people who were told a false story. That loss is part of the pattern, not a neutral ending.
  - *on screen:* The smear outlasts the contact. — Lost relationships are part of the pattern.
