# 06 — Therapeutic Pathways

**Status: ACTIVE (developed 2026-07-09, session `develop-06`)**

## Purpose

Map the self-directed change pathways for the non-violent spectrum — the "treatment"
half that keeps the framework from being purely punitive. The governing constraint is
load-bearing: the person must arrive at the practice *themselves*; externally
prescribed treatment triggers resistance and fails. **This doc's stakes just rose:**
the steelman of the offense draft (OBJ-023) found that criminal law *cannot* reach the
pure-words case (the First Amendment forces non-speech conduct into the offense), so
**for the seed's paradigm case — invisible, words-only coercion — the accommodation
model (doc 05) and *this* doc are the *primary* instruments, not the soft afterthought.**
That makes doc 06's central evidence gap (below) a gap at the framework's core.

## 0. What the evidence actually supports (and the gap it leaves)

From `research/therapeutic-modalities-evidence.md`:

- **The modalities work — for the wrong population.** Mindfulness structural change
  (Hölzel/Lazar, 8-week MBSR gray-matter changes) and the first IFS RCT (PARTS, Harvard/
  Cambridge Health Alliance, PTSD) are real — but the evidence base is almost entirely
  **trauma survivors and general stressed populations**, i.e., the framework's
  *victims*, **not** the people who *enact* the coercive pattern. Applying
  victim-side efficacy to the perpetrator-side deficit is an **unvalidated transfer.**
  `[SUPPORTED]` for wellbeing/trauma; `[HYPOTHESIS]` for remediating the cluster.
- **This is doc 06's weakest load-bearing element** (see below), and — post-steelman —
  it sits at the framework's center of gravity.

## 1. The pathways, re-tagged against evidence

1. **Mindfulness practice (Siegel/MBSR).** `[ESTABLISHED]` that sustained practice
   produces structural brain change in 8 weeks (faster for some measures); `[HYPOTHESIS]`
   that it remediates *this* deficit or builds theory-of-mind "for the first time."
   Keep the mechanism, drop the mPFC-specific + "decade" overstatement (tag-drift /
   OBJ-002).
2. **IFS parts work.** `[SUPPORTED]` (emerging RCT evidence) as trauma therapy;
   `[HYPOTHESIS]` for the coercive-pattern population (no direct evidence).
3. **Personal mythology / esoteric framing (Jessa Reed).** `[HYPOTHESIS]`, n=1,
   illustrative — a self-authored container to metabolize self-awareness without
   triggering defenses. Stays as texture, not evidence.
4. **Self-arrival constraint.** `[ESTABLISHED]` reactance base; `[HYPOTHESIS]` strong
   form. Vindicated: externally imposed change increases defensiveness; self-generated
   motivation is more durable.

## 2. The missing lever the seed lacked — Motivational Interviewing (MI)

`[ESTABLISHED]` (for addiction/health behavior) / `[HYPOTHESIS]` (for this population).
MI is the evidence-based method for eliciting **self-directed** change in
low-insight/resistant people: it reduces reactance by having the person **articulate
their own dissonance** rather than being argued at (directive confrontation is
*iatrogenic* for high-resistance people). It was developed in **addiction** — the
closest denial-dominant analogue to the coercive-pattern population.

**Why it matters:** MI converts the self-arrival constraint from a dead end into a
*trainable, deployable method* — a stance clinicians, and even structured settings, can
adopt without triggering the resistance that defeats imposed treatment. **This is the
partial answer to OBJ-005** ("a self-directed-only cure gives policy nothing to do"):
policy can train and fund an MI-informed stance, create conditions and access, and
*wait* — without prescribing or coercing. MI is the operational core of doc 06.

## Weakest load-bearing element (per `CLAUDE.md` agreement 1)

**The wrong-population evidence gap — now at the framework's center.** Every modality's
efficacy evidence is for trauma *survivors* / general populations, not for the people
who *enact* the coercive pattern. Ordinarily that would make doc 06 a hopeful-but-thin
appendix. But the steelman (OBJ-023) established that criminal law cannot reach the
pure-words case, so docs 05–06 are now the *primary* instruments for the seed's
paradigm harm — which means the framework's central remedy for its central case rests
on an **unvalidated transfer**. This is the single most important thing to be honest
about: *we do not have evidence that these pathways remediate the coercive pattern in
those who enact it*, and the self-arrival + low-insight constraints make the population
hard to study or reach. MI narrows the gap (a method, not just a hope) but does not
close it. The framework must present doc 06 as **principled and promising, not proven**,
and must not let victim-side evidence launder the perpetrator-side claim (tag drift).

## Open questions

- **The self-only paradox — partly answered.** MI (§2) gives policy something to do
  (train the stance, create access, wait) without imposing treatment. Residual: MI's
  own evidence is addiction/health, not this population.
- **Capacity vs. deficit / "non-responsive."** The pathways assume a developable
  capacity. Cases where it does not develop would seem to need a "non-responsive"
  category — which reintroduces person-typing (doc 07). Can this be handled purely by
  *containment* (doc 05 role design) without ever classifying a person as non-responsive?
- **Coercion boundary.** Court-*ordered* "self-directed" therapy is a contradiction. Can
  an MI-informed stance be offered inside a mandated *setting* (e.g., a diversion
  program) without the mandate itself triggering reactance? Unresolved.
- **Division-of-labor write-back.** The finding that docs 05–06 are primary for the
  words-only case must be written into docs 01, 03, 05 (action item from the steelman).
