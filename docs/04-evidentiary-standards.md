# 04 — Evidentiary Standards

**Status: ACTIVE (developed 2026-07-09, session `develop-04`)**

## Purpose

Specify how invisible injury gets *proven* — the evidentiary and procedural half of
the legal framework. Two jobs: (1) admit and weight the kinds of evidence that
sustained coercive harm actually leaves (therapist testimony, documented patterns,
multimedia records), and (2) reform courtroom/agency procedure so proving the harm
does not itself re-inflict it. Procedural reforms here must reconcile
victim-protection with the accused's confrontation and due-process rights — a
tension owned jointly with doc 07.

**This session's spine: the foreseeability reframing** — the candidate escape from the
OBJ-009/011/015 knot (doc 03's likeliest killer), developed at Endorphin's direction.

## 0. The foreseeability reframing (untying the "serious effect" knot)

The knot (doc 08, OBJ-009/011/015): making "serious effect on the *victim*" a
proof-element is simultaneously the vagueness cure, the 3.4% conviction bottleneck, a
re-traumatization vector, and a penalty on resilient victims. Endorphin's move:
shift the burden from *proving harm to a specific victim* to *the objectively
foreseeable harmfulness of the conduct pattern* — "demonstrate that the clusters of
behaviors are a predictable thing; if so that's an easy thing." It is. Three findings
(see `research/foreseeability-and-risk-assessment.md`):

1. **The foreseeability limb already exists in enacted law.** `[ESTABLISHED]` s.76
   requires the perpetrator **"knows or ought to know"** the behaviour will have a
   serious effect — "ought to know" = *what a reasonable person with the same
   information would know*, an **objective foreseeability test** already on the books.
   The reframing *re-weights* onto this existing limb; it does not invent one.
2. **The pattern's harm is professionally predictable.** `[ESTABLISHED]` The field has
   **validated actuarial instruments predicting serious harm — up to homicide — from
   documented coercive-control patterns**: the **Danger Assessment** (Campbell;
   validated for intimate-partner femicide), **SARA**, **ODARA**, **DASH**,
   **MOSAIC-20** (Graham et al. 2021 systematic review). If a profession has
   *validated* tools that predict the harm from the pattern, the harm is foreseeable
   *as established knowledge* — a reasonable person "ought to know."
3. **Survivor-narrative convergence corroborates predictability.** `[SUPPORTED]`
   (qualitative) Endorphin's "Something Was Wrong" springboard: many independent
   survivors describing the *same tactics → same effects* is the naturalistic version
   of what the instruments formalize (the seed's L162 epistemics), and gives the
   pattern jury-comprehensible texture (addresses OBJ-013).
   *Tested 2026-10-04* (`research/sww-corpus/`, pre-registered): across 26 seasons
   spanning intimate, family, cult, workplace, military, troubled-teen-industry,
   university and medical settings, no setting effect on the function profile was
   detected, and the seasons share a profile beyond control narratives (p = 0.004). But
   "core functions in nearly every case" **failed** as pre-registered for isolation and
   perception control. The corpus is curated (one selection process). Stays `[SUPPORTED]`:
   illustrative, not a base rate. Institutional self-similarity (C2) was **not
   adequately tested**: see the power audit in `research/sww-corpus/README.md`.
4. **Meta-analytic support for the *psychological*-harm nexus.** `[SUPPORTED]`
   (added by evidence-check 2026-07-09, answering OBJ-019) The lethality instruments in
   Finding 2 predict *physical* danger; the foreseeability of *psychological* harm rests
   instead on a **2023 systematic review + meta-analysis** — coercive control moderately
   associated with **PTSD (r = .32)** and depression (r = .27), comparable to physical
   IPV. This is the right anchor for the psychological-effect nexus. **Do not overclaim:**
   r = .32 is *moderate*, so foreseeability of psychological harm is *supported, not
   lethality-grade.* (See `research/evidence-check-2026-07-09.md`.)

### CRITICAL GUARDRAIL — foreseeability must be generic, never a defendant risk-score

- **DO** ground the *generic* "ought to know" standard in the *existence and
  validation* of the instruments, as legislative/adjudicative fact that *this kind of
  conduct* foreseeably causes serious harm.
- **DO NOT** score an individual defendant (e.g., on the Danger Assessment) and offer
  the score as proof they should have foreseen harm. Group base-rates → individual
  culpability is the **"actuarial justice" trap** (*State v. Loomis*, Wis. 2016) — a
  due-process and disparate-impact problem. The instruments were built for *victim
  safety-planning* (err toward over-prediction), not for proving criminal elements
  beyond reasonable doubt. Keep the individual case on documented **conduct**, not a
  **score**. `NEEDS-LIBERTY-REVIEW`.

### Structural consequence for doc 03 (result-crime → endangerment-crime)

Once harm is objectively foreseeable from the pattern, doc 03 can define the offense
as *engaging in the foreseeably-harmful pattern* (an **endangerment** offense, like
DUI — no victim required), with *actual* serious effect demoted from **element** to
**sentencing aggravator**. This dissolves all four horns of the knot. **Named
tradeoff:** endangerment broadens liability (no harm required), so a false accuser
need only allege the *pattern*, not prove *harm* — a both-victims expansion routed to
`/liberty-review`. Whether it is the better trade turns on the conduct pattern being
more objectively documentable, and less re-traumatizing to prove, than subjective
harm (plausibly yes). Logged to doc 03 and OBJ-009/011/015. `[NORMATIVE]`

## Candidate standards & procedures (from the seed)

1. **Therapist / counselor testimony** admissible as evidence of the abuse and the
   victim's psychological state. `[NORMATIVE]` (procedural proposal).
2. **Pattern documentation** — showing a course of conduct over time rather than a
   single incident (aligns with coercive-control charging in doc 03). `[SUPPORTED]`.
3. **Multimedia / "ambient manipulation" records** — recordings, messages, and
   other traces of the deceptive-atmosphere construction. `[HYPOTHESIS]` /
   `[NORMATIVE]` (novel; feasibility and authentication unproven).
4. **Victim-protective procedures** — no forced confrontation with the perpetrator;
   remote testimony; evidence protection. `[SUPPORTED]` (analogues exist in DV and
   child-witness practice).
5. **Trained officials** — police/court/medical personnel trained to recognize
   pattern signs. `[NORMATIVE]`.

## Weakest load-bearing element (per `CLAUDE.md` agreement 1)

**The foreseeability reframing solves the knot by *relocating* the danger, not
removing it.** Shifting from "prove harm to this victim" to "prove the foreseeably-
harmful pattern" (§0) trades a *victim-burdening* problem for a *liberty-broadening*
one: an endangerment offense with no harm requirement is easier to allege and thus
easier to weaponize (the confession-through-accusation inversion). The reframing is
genuinely promising — the predictability is established (Finding 2) and the "ought to
know" limb already exists (Finding 1) — but it is **unproven as a net improvement**
until `/liberty-review` weighs the broadened-liability cost against the
victim-protection gain. Do not present it as a settled fix.

## Open questions

- **Endangerment vs. result-crime.** Is "no harm required" a price worth paying for
  dissolving the knot? Turns on whether documented *conduct* is genuinely more
  provable and less re-traumatizing than subjective *harm*. Route to `/liberty-review`.
- **Generic vs. individual foreseeability.** Can "ought to know" be grounded in the
  instruments' *existence* without any individual defendant ever being risk-scored
  (avoiding the *Loomis* trap)? Drafting problem for doc 03.
- **Confrontation Clause.** Victim-protective procedure (remote testimony, no
  face-to-face) collides with the Sixth Amendment right to confront witnesses. What
  is the constitutionally defensible middle? (Child-witness and DV precedent is the
  starting point.)
- **Authentication of "ambient" records.** How is a recording of a "deceptive
  atmosphere" authenticated, and how is it kept from being fabricated by a false
  accuser (both-victims lens)?
- **Therapist-testimony reliability.** Dr. Durvasula's point (L203) that most
  therapists get ~one week on personality disorders cuts both ways: therapist
  testimony may be probative *or* may import untrained pattern-projection. What
  qualifies an expert here?
- **Pattern vs. prejudice.** How does "course of conduct" evidence avoid becoming
  propensity evidence (generally inadmissible) about the accused's character?
