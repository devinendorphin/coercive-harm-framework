# 04 — Evidentiary Standards

**Status: STUB (seeded, undeveloped)**

## Purpose

Specify how invisible injury gets *proven* — the evidentiary and procedural half of
the legal framework. Two jobs: (1) admit and weight the kinds of evidence that
sustained coercive harm actually leaves (therapist testimony, documented patterns,
multimedia records), and (2) reform courtroom/agency procedure so proving the harm
does not itself re-inflict it. Procedural reforms here must reconcile
victim-protection with the accused's confrontation and due-process rights — a
tension owned jointly with doc 07.

## Candidate standards & procedures (from the seed)

1. **Therapist / counselor testimony** admissible as evidence of the abuse and the
   victim's psychological state. `[NORMATIVE]` (procedural proposal).
2. **Pattern documentation** — showing a course of conduct over time rather than a
   single incident (aligns with coercive-control charging in doc 03). `[SUPPORTED]`.
3. **Multimedia / "ambient manipulation" records** — recordings, messages, and
   other traces of the deceptive-atmosphere construction. `[HYPOTHESIS]` /
   `[NORMATIVE]` (novel; feasibility and authentication unproven).
4. **Victim-protective procedures** — no forced confrontation with the perpetrator;
   remote testimony; evidence protection. `[SUPPORTED]` (analogues exist in DV and
   child-witness practice).
5. **Trained officials** — police/court/medical personnel trained to recognize
   pattern signs. `[NORMATIVE]`.

## Relevant passages from the seed

- **Testimony + documentation** (~L11, L21): "allowing testimony from
  counselors/therapists treating victims"; "documentation of the abuser's
  manipulative tactics as admissible in court"; "evidence of the abuse victim's
  psychological state."
- **Records as evidence for homicide theory** (~L31): "allowing victims' previous
  statements, therapy records, etc. to be admitted as evidence that the abuser
  engaged in calculated long-term conditioning."
- **Re-traumatization by procedure** (~L91): the victim "has to then retraumatize
  herself... narrat[ing] over and over again... to an enforcement apparatus [that]
  does not believe her," and procedures that "have the perpetrator and the victim
  in the same room together which is... traumatizing."
- **Asymmetry of endurance** (~L91): confrontation is "not as nausea[ting]" for the
  perpetrator, so the procedure selects against genuine victims. *(Evidentiary
  design implication: confrontation-based process is a biased instrument here.)*
- **New evidentiary standards, carefully** (~L114–L115): "new evidentiary standards
  like multimedia records of ambient manipulation"; "due process protections like
  specialized advocacy for both parties."

## Open questions

- **Confrontation Clause.** Victim-protective procedure (remote testimony, no
  face-to-face) collides with the Sixth Amendment right to confront witnesses. What
  is the constitutionally defensible middle? (Child-witness and DV precedent is the
  starting point.)
- **Authentication of "ambient" records.** How is a recording of a "deceptive
  atmosphere" authenticated, and how is it kept from being fabricated by a false
  accuser (both-victims lens)?
- **Therapist-testimony reliability.** Dr. Durvasula's point (L203) that most
  therapists get ~one week on personality disorders cuts both ways: therapist
  testimony may be probative *or* may import untrained pattern-projection. What
  qualifies an expert here?
- **Pattern vs. prejudice.** How does "course of conduct" evidence avoid becoming
  propensity evidence (generally inadmissible) about the accused's character?

## Research needed

- **Confrontation Clause** accommodations already upheld: Maryland v. Craig
  (child-witness CCTV), DV remote-testimony practice, protective-order evidentiary
  standards.
- **Expert-testimony admissibility** (Daubert/Frye) for psychological-abuse and
  coercive-control expert witnesses; battered-woman-syndrome testimony history as a
  precedent and cautionary tale.
- **Course-of-conduct evidence** rules in stalking and coercive-control
  prosecutions; how other jurisdictions handle pattern proof without propensity
  problems.
- **Digital-evidence authentication** standards relevant to messages/recordings.
- **Dr. Ramani Durvasula's** published work on clinician training gaps (L203) —
  find the primary sources behind the "one-week unit" claim.
