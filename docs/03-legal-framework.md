# 03 — Legal Framework

**Status: ACTIVE (developed 2026-07-09, session `develop-03`)**
**Every mechanism below carries `NEEDS-LIBERTY-REVIEW` until `/liberty-review` clears it.**

## Purpose

Propose the new injury category, an identification rubric, and a graduated response
model (punitive ↔ rehabilitative), plus the consent-law consolidation the seed
calls for. This is the workstream where the framework is most dangerous to get
wrong: mechanisms strong enough to reach invisible harm are strong enough to be
weaponized, and the seed's own warning (L107) governs. Nothing here is stable until
it passes doc 07's review.

---

## 0. What the law actually does today (grounding — verified 2026-07)

Before proposing new law, three facts from `research/coercive-control-statutes.md`
and `research/coerced-suicide-prosecutions.md` that discipline everything below:

1. **Enacted coercive-control law is definitionally domestic.** Every criminal
   coercive-control statute (England & Wales SCA 2015 s.76; Scotland 2018; Ireland
   2018) gates to parties who are "personally connected" — intimate partners, former
   partners who cohabit, or family. None reaches workplace, institutional, or
   governmental relationships. [ESTABLISHED]
2. **US criminalization barely exists.** Hawaii is the *only* US state with a
   standalone criminal coercive-control offense — a **petty misdemeanor**, 2021,
   5-year pilot. Every other US state addresses coercive control **civilly**:
   protective-order definitions and child-custody "best interest" factors
   (Connecticut's "Jennifer's Law" 2021; California Family Code). [ESTABLISHED]
3. **The flagship criminal model barely convicts.** England & Wales charged **3.4%
   of 41,626 recorded CCB cases** (year to March 2022). The bottleneck is the
   statutory "serious effect on the victim" proof requirement. [ESTABLISHED]

**Design consequence:** the framework should stop treating *criminalization* as the
default lever. The enacted, working machinery is largely **civil** (protective
orders, custody factors) and **employment** (accommodation — doc 05), scale-matched
to the relationship. Criminal law is the exception, reserved for the gravest,
most-provable conduct. This reframes the mechanisms below from "new crimes" to "a
scale-matched portfolio of instruments, mostly civil."

---

## 1. The scale-matched portfolio (reframed "new injury category")

Rather than one new injury category enforced one way, the framework needs **one
named harm** recognized across **several scale-appropriate instruments**, because
the harm occurs in relationship contexts that different bodies of law govern:

| Scale / relationship | Instrument | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Intimate / family | Civil protective order (expanded definition) → criminal offense for gravest cases | `[SUPPORTED]` (enacted models exist) |
| Workplace / institutional | ADA-style accommodation + anti-harassment/employment law (→ doc 05) | `[NORMATIVE]` |
| Governmental / geopolitical | *No individual-harm instrument exists or is proposed* | out of legal scope (see §7) |

This portfolio framing is the direct, disciplined consequence of the **bounded**
cross-scale claim (§7). `NEEDS-LIBERTY-REVIEW` for each instrument.

### 1a. The relationship-gate gap: networked households (metamour/polycule abuse)

The "intimate/family" gate is not just *scale*-limited (§7) — it is *topology*-
limited, and it misses the seed's **founding example.** Under the Domestic Abuse Act
2021 §2, "personally connected" is a closed list: intimate partners, ex-partners,
family members, or co-parents of a child. A **metamour** — one's partner's other
partner, two people connected *through* a shared partner but not themselves
partnered or related — falls into **none** of those categories. Abuse running
*across* a multi-partner household network, rather than *down* a two-person
intimate dyad, is therefore outside the coercive-control offense entirely.
[ESTABLISHED — confirmed against DA Act 2021 §2]

This is exactly the seed's first case (L40): a "five-person apartment[,] two
couples," where Endorphin was "the primary devalued for both of the abusive halves
of that apartment." The harm was **networked, not dyadic** — and the statute that
exists to reach coercive control would not reach it, because the gate is drawn
around the couple, not the household. [the framework's own seed case is uncovered]

- **Do not overclaim:** metamour abuse might still be charged under ordinary
  **harassment/stalking** statutes. But those are **incident-based**, not
  course-of-conduct — which drops the victim straight back into the invisible-injury
  problem the coercive-control offense was invented to solve. So the gap is real
  *specifically for the pattern-of-conduct framing.* [SUPPORTED]
- **Design implication:** the identification rubric (§1) should gate on the
  **structure of the relationship and the conduct** (a shared household / sustained
  interdependence + a documented course of controlling conduct), not on a closed
  list of dyad types. This must be liberty-reviewed carefully: widening the gate is
  exactly where both-victims and vagueness risks grow. `NEEDS-LIBERTY-REVIEW`
  `[NORMATIVE]` (logged as OBJ-008).

## 2. Coercive-control offense — civil-first, criminal-exceptional

- The **course-of-conduct** structure (pattern over time, not incident-by-incident)
  is the correct core and is enacted law abroad. [SUPPORTED]
- Given the 3.4% charge rate and the "serious effect" bottleneck, the framework
  should **lead with the civil instrument** (US states already do) and treat a
  *criminal* coercive-control offense as reserved for grave, well-evidenced cases.
  This lowers vagueness and due-process stakes (doc 07) while still recognizing the
  harm. `NEEDS-LIBERTY-REVIEW` `[NORMATIVE]`
- The enacted **"serious effect on the victim"** element is worth adopting: it
  centers *impact on the victim* over *motive of the perpetrator* (matching seed
  L113) — while honestly noting it is *also* the conviction bottleneck. We inherit
  the tension, we don't dissolve it. [SUPPORTED]

## 3. "Psychological / coercive homicide" — aspiration, not a chargeable offense yet

- **Real toehold:** *Commonwealth v. Carter* (Mass. 2019, cert denied 2020)
  sustained an **involuntary manslaughter** conviction for words that coerced a
  suicide, and **rejected the First Amendment "pure speech" defense** ("cannot escape
  liability just because she happened to use words"). *Commonwealth v. You* pushes
  toward a *pattern*-of-coercion theory (disposition unconfirmed). [ESTABLISHED /
  SUPPORTED]
- **But the enacted reality is narrow:** active, contemporaneous encouragement of an
  *imminent, specific* suicide — not the seed's slow-burn erosion over months/years
  (L26). Two walls stand between them:
  - **Causation.** Manslaughter needs but-for + proximate cause to the death. A
    years-long erosion with many intervening causes (addiction, other stressors, the
    victim's own agency) is far harder to pin than a real-time "get back in the
    truck." [the core obstacle]
  - **Mens rea.** Carter turned on a finding she *wanted* him dead. If the cluster
    conduct is genuinely reactive/improvisational (doc 02), specific intent may be
    unavailable; a recklessness/wanton-conduct theory is the better fit but still
    needs a provable act-to-death chain slow-burn cases lack.
- **Verdict for the doc:** keep "psychological homicide" as a **named aspiration
  requiring doctrinal invention**, explicitly *not* presented as chargeable today.
  Tag stays `[SUPPORTED]` (narrow pattern) + `[NORMATIVE]` (the extension).
  `NEEDS-LIBERTY-REVIEW` — high priority (speech-boundary + due-process).

## 4. Consent-law consolidation around force / fraud / fear

- Endorphin's L8 claim — consent law is "scattered and messy" — **survives contact
  with the statute.** NY coercion (Penal Law §135.65) is degree-graded and anchored
  in **fear of physical injury or property damage** (the "damage to property" prong
  is the "arson" he half-remembered), with **no unified force/fraud/fear test** and
  little reach over fraud or non-threat manipulation. Re-tag his claim
  HYPOTHESIS → `[SUPPORTED]`. (See `research/consent-law-force-fraud-fear.md`.)
- Force/fraud/fear are **established** organizing elements in adjacent law
  (trafficking; sexual offenses). [ESTABLISHED]
- The **consolidation** — a coherent force/fraud/fear spine *across* consent contexts
  — is a `[NORMATIVE]` reform proposal. The **fraud** prong is the danger zone: it is
  precisely where "deception that isn't currently illegal" (seed L16) becomes
  actionable, and where vagueness + both-victims risks concentrate.
  `NEEDS-LIBERTY-REVIEW` — the fraud prong especially.

## 5. Graduated punitive ↔ rehabilitative response

- Non-violent conduct routes toward accommodation (doc 05) and self-directed
  pathways (doc 06); grave/violent conduct toward the criminal instruments above.
  The civil-first posture (§2) *is* the front end of this graduation. `[NORMATIVE]`
  `NEEDS-LIBERTY-REVIEW`

## 6. Expedited track — RECOMMEND DROPPING

- The seed itself flagged this as "a minefield" (L102), and OBJ-003 names it the
  framework's most likely killer. The research makes the case against it **stronger,
  not weaker**: even ordinary domestic CCB charges only 3.4% of cases *because proof
  is genuinely hard* — an "expedited" track would either (a) lower the proof standard
  for a class of hard-to-disprove accusations (a gift to false accusers — the exact
  confession-through-accusation inversion the framework exists to stop), or (b) add
  speed without lowering proof, in which case it accomplishes nothing the research
  shows is the actual constraint.
- **Recommendation:** remove the expedited-track mechanism from the framework, or
  narrow it to *procedural victim-protection* (priority scheduling, remote testimony
  — doc 04) with **no** alteration of the burden of proof. Flag for Endorphin's call.
  `NEEDS-LIBERTY-REVIEW` → provisionally `BLOCKED` pending redesign.

## 7. Resolution: is the cross-scale claim load-bearing? (the session's assigned question)

**Answer: it bifurcates. The bounded form is load-bearing; the universalizing form
is severable and should be severed.**

- **Bounded two-rung claim — LOAD-BEARING `[SUPPORTED]`.** "The same conduct pattern
  occurs in *domestic* relationships *and* in *workplace/institutional* ones." This
  is what makes the framework a **portfolio** (§1) rather than a footnote to existing
  domestic-violence law. The law compels the split: criminal/civil coercive-control
  instruments are *domestic-only* by statute, so the workplace manifestation *must*
  be reached by a *different* instrument (ADA/employment — doc 05). Remove this claim
  and doc 05 loses its entire justification and the framework collapses to "enforce
  existing DV law." Endorphin's instinct that it is load-bearing is **correct in this
  form.**

- **Strong universalizing claim — ORNAMENTAL and hazardous `[HYPOTHESIS]`.**
  "Identical tactics at *every* scale up through geopolitics, regardless of
  race/gender/age/disability, challenging the notion of the unique individual"
  (L59). No legal mechanism reaches the governmental/international rung as an
  individual-harm instrument; it is legally inert. And its universalism ("the same,
  regardless of all difference") is exactly the pressure that tempts the framework
  toward naming a *type/trait* — the person-typing doc 07 forbids — and is what
  OBJ-007 says will discredit the project.

- **What to do:** In doc 01, demote the geopolitical/universal rung from structural
  premise to **motivating vision**, explicitly labeled non-load-bearing, and promote
  the **household↔workplace** span to a `[SUPPORTED]` structural premise that the
  legal portfolio depends on. This *strengthens* the framework: it keeps the rung the
  mechanisms actually stand on and sheds the rung that only draws fire. (Action item
  logged to doc 01 and OBJ-007.)

## Weakest load-bearing element (per `CLAUDE.md` agreement 1)

**The psychological-homicide mechanism (§3) is the weakest thing still standing.**
Not because the harm isn't real, but because the causation wall is doctrinally
severe and the framework has an incentive to overclaim here — "slow-burn homicide"
is rhetorically the most powerful phrase in the whole project and the least
supported by enacted law. If a critic wants to make the framework look like activist
overreach, §3 is where they'll aim, and Carter's narrow, contested holding won't
carry the weight the seed's language puts on it. Recommend §3 be the first thing
`/steelman` (prosecutor + defense-attorney personas) and `/liberty-review`
(speech-boundary) attack.
