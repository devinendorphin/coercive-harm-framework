# 00 — Glossary

Terms of art coined or adopted by Endorphin in the April 2024 seed conversation
(`source/seed-conversation-2024.txt`), plus a few the framework needs to name its
own guardrails. Each definition is faithful to the seed's usage and carries an
epistemic-status tag per `CLAUDE.md`:
`[ESTABLISHED]` `[SUPPORTED]` `[HYPOTHESIS]` `[NORMATIVE]`.

A tag on a *term* marks the status of the claim the term smuggles in — the naming
is free, but "psychological homicide" asserts a causal chain, and that chain is
what gets tagged. Where a term bundles several claims at different strengths, the
tag reflects its load-bearing one and the entry says so.

---

### clusters of behaviors
`[HYPOTHESIS]`
Endorphin's preferred umbrella term for the recurring pattern of conduct the whole
project targets — chosen deliberately over any diagnostic or personality label
(seed L4, L36, L40, L80). It denotes *documented patterns of conduct over time*:
confession-through-accusation, truth-inversion, seeding chaos, performed empathy,
isolation of a target from support. The term is doing real work as a
person-neutral alternative to "narcissist" / "Cluster B" (which Endorphin uses
only reluctantly, as "practical shorthand," L162). That the enumerated behaviors
co-occur as a coherent *cluster* is the hypothesis; individual behaviors within it
are separately better-supported.

### confession through accusation
`[SUPPORTED]`
"When a problematic personality is accusing you of something [it] is generally of
the very thing that they are doing to that person" (L80). Endorphin treats this as
"the fingerprint" of the cluster. This is the psychological mechanism of
**projection**, which has clinical and empirical literature; the specific
"reliably diagnostic fingerprint" claim — that accusation content reverse-maps to
the accuser's own conduct with enough regularity to be evidentiarily useful — is
the part that is thinner and needs research. Tagged SUPPORTED for the mechanism,
HYPOTHESIS for the diagnostic-reliability upgrade.

### inversion of truth / "everything is the inverse"
`[HYPOTHESIS]`
The claim that a chronic deceiver, to conserve the cognitive energy of tracking
many lies, defaults to a simple rule — invert what is true — so that "everything
that they say is the inverse but you only can know which that is by how they say
it" (L59). Presented by Endorphin as a decoding heuristic. The energy-conservation
rationale is plausible and evocative but untested; the "only knowable by *how*
they say it" clause is what keeps this from being an evidentiary rule.

### deceptive atmosphere
`[HYPOTHESIS]`
The manufactured ambient field a perpetrator creates around a target — the sense
that reality itself has been subtly bent — such that "the faculties of mind are
being overridden by changing the ambient field around a person in a certain way"
(L59, L102). Related to but broader than gaslighting: it names the *environmental*,
not merely dyadic, quality of the manipulation. Overlaps with interpersonal
neurobiology framings (mirror-neuron exploitation, L102).

### weaponization of words (and concepts)
`[SUPPORTED]`
The foundational premise: "there... can be a weaponization of words and concepts
[such] that those concepts and words can also actually have a concrete physical
effect on the body" (L16, L24). That sustained psychological abuse and coercive
control produce measurable physiological and neurological harm is
well-established; the framing of specific words/narratives as the *weapon* is the
rhetorical frame the legal workstream must operationalize into conduct-based
language.

### psychological homicide / coercive homicide / "slow-burn homicide"
`[SUPPORTED]` + `[NORMATIVE]`
The theory that some deaths recorded as suicide or substance-abuse deaths are
better understood as the terminal outcome of sustained coercive abuse — a
"slow burn homicide facilitated by the perpetrator, who has managed to embed a
narrative amongst the victim's family and friends that she succumbed to her
demons" (L26, L30). The causal link between coercive control and suicidality is
SUPPORTED (see Michelle Carter / Conrad Roy; UK coercive-control prosecutions);
whether that link should ground a *homicide* charge is a NORMATIVE / policy
position, not an empirical finding. Kept as two tags on purpose.

### seeds of chaos
`[HYPOTHESIS]`
Preemptively planted failure points a perpetrator can "trip at any time when that
moment needs to happen" — built into a system in advance "but they also do it
improvisationally," so that projects under their influence "fail on multiple
levels[,] Grand Cascades of misfortune" (L80). Names a sabotage pattern; the
claim that it is a deliberate-yet-deniable engineered feature is Endorphin's
theory.

### improvised-but-convergent manipulation (reactionary improvisation)
`[HYPOTHESIS]`
A key nuance: what looks "seemingly coordinated" is not premeditated conspiracy
but "done very reactionary... as a response to threat stimuli and thus it seems
coordinated but is actually all improvisational" — executed with skill exceeding
"most [of] humanity" because it is a lifelong substitute "in place of creating
simulations of other people in their head" (L49). Explains cross-scale similarity
(L59) without requiring collusion. This nuance matters legally: it cuts against
"conspiracy" framings and toward pattern-of-conduct framings.

### performed empathy / performed attunement
`[HYPOTHESIS]`
The pattern of convincingly *displaying* emotional attunement and perspective-taking
while lacking the underlying capacity — "they say they [are] the Masters at it and
then they end up not, but they know... that's going to happen" (L80). At the
beginning of a relationship the target "feel[s] seen" (L16); the performance later
inverts into a weapon. Central to the accommodation and therapeutic workstreams.

### mPFC-deficit hypothesis (theory-of-mind / seat-of-morality)
`[HYPOTHESIS]`
The seed's central neurological conjecture: that the cluster reflects a deficit at
the **medial prefrontal cortex**, described as "the huge integrator" that regulates
emotion, seats morality ("the striving of a greater good even with no one
watching"), enables attunement, and lets one "create simulations of other people
in your head" (L80). Explicitly a hypothesis — the mPFC's role in these functions
is real and studied, but attributing *this behavioral cluster* to *this structural
deficit* is unvalidated and, per `/steelman`, the framework's single most
attackable and most stigmatizing claim. Handle with maximum epistemic discipline.

### vulnaflex
`[NORMATIVE]`
Endorphin's self-coined portmanteau — a "flex... of vulnerability" (L2) — naming
the stance of the whole project: doing serious, exposed, self-implicating work in
the open. Not a claim about the world; a description of method and voice. Preserved
because it captures the register `CLAUDE.md` asks us not to sterilize.

### force / fraud / fear
`[ESTABLISHED]`
The tripartite test Endorphin wants consent law to consolidate around, contrasted
with New York's "scattered and very messy" consent statutes that sometimes "[don't]
even regard sexual assault" (L8). Force/fraud/fear (and coercion) are established
elements across various coercion and trafficking statutes; the NORMATIVE move is
*consolidating* consent doctrine around them. Verify the specific NY statutory
claims in `/develop 03`.

### honest-capacity-statement culture (inability to state capacities)
`[SUPPORTED]`
The named cultural deficit behind the accommodation workstream: "a tendency at
least with Americans to not be able to state their capacities and especially not
be able to state the[ir] limitations for fear [of] being seen as weakness" (L118).
The workplace remedy Endorphin models is an "administrative template" that assigns
roles honestly around capacity (e.g., "no financial strings," "no one under her,"
L118). The cultural observation is well-attested in organizational literature; the
specific template is a NORMATIVE design proposal.

### self-arrival constraint (self-directed-only change)
`[HYPOTHESIS]`
The core therapeutic constraint: change "can't be something that... a pill... [or]
cognitive behavioral therapy [where] another person is involved" — "not only they
have to do it alone, they have to think of the idea themselves, because if anyone
in their lives that they have beef with says 'here's a solution,' the last thing
that they will do is take that advice" (L118). Reactance is a real, established
phenomenon; the strong form (change is impossible unless self-originated) is the
hypothesis, and it sits in tension with any *mandated* accommodation or treatment —
a tension the framework has not yet resolved.

### personal mythology / esoteric framing (the Jessa Reed case)
`[HYPOTHESIS]`
Using a self-authored symbolic narrative — Reed's "being encountered by a space
alien" (L147) — as a non-dogmatic "container" to metabolize painful self-awareness
without triggering defenses. Endorphin ties this to American/European esoteric
traditions as "the way in which humans tried to make meaning outside of organized
religion" (L147), while explicitly wanting to keep it "grounded in... the
empirical" and not "too mystical" (L177). A candidate self-directed pathway, n=1.

### cross-scale pattern (household → workplace → institution → geopolitics)
`[HYPOTHESIS]`
The claim that the same cluster recurs, with the same tactics, at every level of
social organization — interpersonal, workplace, nonprofit, municipal, national,
international — "regardless of race[,] ethnicity[,] gender[,] age[,] disability"
(L36, L59, L69). Endorphin's own examples span a five-person household, an arts
role, a Flatbush mental-health agency, and geopolitics (L40, L69, L276). The
"same tactics at every scale" claim is a strong structural hypothesis; the
Flatbush example is also a deliberate reminder that power-abuse and enforcement
bias cross all community lines (feeds `/liberty-review` disparate-impact prong).

## Guardrail terms (framework's own vocabulary)

### both-victims lens
`[NORMATIVE]`
The standing requirement that any mechanism strong enough to protect victims of
invisible harm is strong enough to be weaponized against innocent people — rooted
in the seed's own warning that "explicitly delineating characteristics could
potentially resemble or enable discriminatory practices, which must be avoided at
all costs" (L107), and in the recognition that false accusation *is itself* a
core cluster tactic (confession through accusation). Every legal mechanism passes
`/liberty-review` before being marked stable.

### behavior-not-people rule
`[NORMATIVE]`
All taxonomy and legal language targets documented patterns of conduct, never
diagnostic categories, personality labels, or innate/neurological characteristics.
Directly enforces the seed's civil-liberties constraint (L107, L112–L113) and
guards against the mPFC hypothesis leaking from explanation into classification.

### tag drift
`[NORMATIVE]`
The failure mode `/evidence-check` exists to catch: writing about a `[HYPOTHESIS]`
in the confident grammatical register of established fact. Named as a term because
it is the single most likely way this framework discredits itself with expert
readers.
