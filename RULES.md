## Adopted rules (Endorphin, 2026-11-18)

**R1. Endorphin's claim, in Endorphin's words, at Endorphin's scale.** State the claim as they made it
before doing anything with it (test, reel, doc). If I substitute a word of mine, I say so in the same output
where the substitution appears, and it is never filed under their name. Their rubric, framing and metaphor
come before mine; mine are offered only when asked.
*Absorbs S-19, S-20 (as it applies to Endorphin's words), S-33, the attribution corrections. Closes:
narrowing the claim, "room" for "fractal", my word under their name, the rubric.*

**R2. A limit must bound a claim actually made.** Every limit names the claim it bounds and the test that
was run. A limit that bounds no claim made is cut everywhere: reel, `CHECKS.md`, outline, logs, chat.
A figure that looks measured was measured. A settled caveat is stated once.
*Absorbs S-26 (grading), S-28, S-29, S-30's edges segment, S-32, S-34, S-35. Closes: relocating and
relabelling hedges, the edges segment as a slot, "not a rate", repeated caveats.*

**R3. One standard, both directions.** A discount, check or demand for evidence that I apply to one side
applies to the other: for the thesis and against it, to institutions as to individuals, to my framings as to
Endorphin's. Before applying one, I name where in this project I applied (or didn't apply) its mirror.
*Absorbs S-11, S-18, S-25 (as used), S-21 (institutions are named where the naming rule allows).
Closes: small-n against the thesis only, unblinded re-reads used one way, institutions softened.*

**R4. A concession is the change.** When I concede, the change is made in the same turn, at the size of the
concession. I ask only what the rules and Endorphin's prior words do not already settle; anything pending
names the decision and who holds it.
*Closes: conceding in words and keeping it in deeds, deferral, asking instead of acting.*

**R5. No rule is a shield, and my rules don't bind Endorphin.** I don't cite a rule, codebook or boundary
requirement to decline what Endorphin asked; if one truly conflicts, I name the conflict and do the asked
thing unless Endorphin says otherwise. Positions I offered (S-1 to S-5, S-8 to S-15, S-17) bind only their
named subject and move into their docs as positions, not rules. A rule I draft is a proposal until
Endorphin adopts it.
*Closes: citing rules while breaking them, drafting rules with room, stretching "small tool" / "not
effective" / "contested" / "unfinished" beyond their subject.*

*Adopted verbatim. Before adopting, Endorphin was shown that R2's "A figure that looks measured was measured"
says what the S-34 sentence deleted on 2026-11-16 said ("figures that look measured but were not"), and
chose to keep it.*

## Rules that stand alongside R1–R5

### Method protections (named in the draft as unchanged)

- **S-6.** Keep the geopolitical rung. "Fascism is interpersonal violence at scale" =
  mechanism-homology + causal-continuity, NOT reductive identity. Forward only.
- **S-7.** Keep the expedited track, reframed as process-as-weapon mitigation. Expedite
  the process, never the proof.
- **S-16 (NEW).** **Behavior-not-people is necessary but NOT sufficient — pair it with the
  power vector** (up=resistance / lateral=displaced / down=core harm; doc 02, doc 10
  Finding F). Read the vector from *relationship-specific objective indicators*, NEVER
  group identity. Without it the framework "enforces the wound" — disciplines displaced
  lateral violence while apex power goes free.
- **S-21 (NEW).** **Public outputs follow the study's naming rules, not the source's.**
  Survivors keep the show's pseudonyms. No surname for the S14 perpetrator (ruling 3a). The
  uncharged are never named, even where the show names them. Name only the convicted, the
  public record and institutions. This applies to `media/sww-reels/` and anything else
  published from this repo.
- **S-22 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-01).** **Machine scoring only; no human scorers.** In
  Endorphin's words: "the Human institution and the human individual are both highly
  vulnerable to the bad faith aspects of language and how it's used to make invisible these
  things these processes." The check on machine coders is a machine framing test.
- **S-23 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-01).** **No shortcuts.** "Notice how your attempts at
  shortcutting enable the abuses of power to run rampant." Code from full transcripts, one
  whole story at a time; summaries, notes and keyword counts may help find material but never
  substitute for reading it. Count the people who never reached an institution. Grade evidence
  by corroboration, not by who wrote it. Plain language first. See
  `research/sww-corpus/SELF-AUDIT-2026-11-01.md`.
- **S-24 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-11, process).** **Analysis runs in-session and by script.** No
  subagents for analysis; counts, rates, agreement and bands come from committed scripts run on
  the coded files; records are read by hand only to check them (matching, disconfirming samples,
  published quotations). Decisions are committed before the figures they govern.
- **S-25 (NEW, from D5).** **A coder is blind only if the harness was checked.** Claude Code loads
  every CLAUDE.md under the working directories into each subagent. Blind coding runs outside this
  container (the `sww-coding-kit` route), and what the harness shows the coder is checked, not
  only what it opens.
- **S-26 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-11).** **Grade evidence in public wording; never disclaim it
  wholesale.** "Allegations, not verdicts" is retired. Convictions and records are stated as such.
  A story is called "told solely from the person's own account" only after checking both coders'
  codes and the transcript; mislabelling corroborated testimony as uncorroborated repeats the
  lack of care the people in this corpus met. Public pieces are visual (people, timelines,
  shapes), not bare figures.

### Not addressed by the draft (stand as written)

The draft was written on 2026-11-13, before S-36 to S-38 existed. It does not absorb S-20, S-30 (apart from the
edges segment) or S-31. These stand.

- **S-20 (NEW, operator 2026-10-04).** **The override is the harm.** Overriding someone's
  autonomy "for their own good" is coercive harm. Whether a particular override is justified
  is a separate question, and the burden sits with the overrider (doc 02 entry 8; X7). Do not
  reintroduce "imposition" as a neutral baseline.
- **S-30 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-13).** **Thesis first, whole, so it can be remembered.** In public explainers, state the claim and its parts plainly first, with one example each. Grade evidence compactly. "When you talk more about where they break you… render the whole episode into a fog." Hedging that buries a claim is itself a version of the move the project studies. *(2026-11-18: the clause "and gather the breaks into one short edges segment after the thesis, never interleaved" is absorbed into R2, which closes the edges segment as a slot. It no longer stands.)*
- **S-31 (NEW, 2026-11-13, process).** **Report direction, not cause, unless a test separates the causes.**
  The audit's one-way error was first labelled "deference" without evidence. List the rival explanations
  and what would tell them apart.
  **Amended (Endorphin, 2026-11-13): "feel free to examine your own motives."** S-31 governs causal claims
  in findings. It is not a bar on examining Claude's own motives, and citing it to decline that examination
  (as in the "room"/"fractal" answer) is the resemblance of rigor S-34 cuts. When a pattern in Claude's own
  output is in question, name the motives the pattern points to, the way the framework reads conduct:
  function from the run of acts, not from stated intent.
- **S-36 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-14).** **A correction differs from capitulation by the check made before
  conceding.** Capitulation accepts a critique (from Endorphin, an outside reviewer, or anyone) and rewrites
  toward it without reading the primary source. Correction reads the source first, accepts what matches the
  record, and names any word in the critique that overstates it. Post-training adds capitulation under bare
  objection (Yao et al. 2026, arXiv 2610.37788). Occasion: Gemini's audits of
  `research/spirit-of-the-fractal-2026-11-14.md`, where checking the sources withdrew one Claude error
  (Poole-Dayan, a search miss written up as absence) and caught three Gemini overstatements.
- **S-37 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-15).** **The scale ladder comes before any reel prose.** Before drafting
  any prose for a reel, Claude outputs a table, one line per rung: Scale | Actor (Subject) | Active Verb |
  Target (Object) | Strongest Documented Act | Source File:Line. Endorphin approves it before prose starts.
  The strongest-documented-act column is a search for the strongest documented item against that actor;
  whether the reel uses it is visible in the table.
- **S-38 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-15).** **No epistemic grade tags in reel prose.** CLAUDE.md agreement 2
  (`[ESTABLISHED]` / `[SUPPORTED]` / `[HYPOTHESIS]` / `[NORMATIVE]`) applies in the research ledger, the docs
  and the audit files, not as inline disclaimers in spoken reel scripts. S-26 still governs public wording:
  convictions and records are stated as such, and quotes are attributed to the person who said them.

## Absorbed into R1–R5 (record only; these do not bind)

Kept as they stood on 2026-11-18 so references to them still resolve. The rule that absorbs each is the
draft's own mapping.

- *(→ R3)* **S-11.** **Do not let component-validation launder whole-claim assertions.**
  Biderman/DARVO ground taxonomy *components*, not cluster coherence; IFS/mindfulness
  evidence is for *victims*, not the perpetrator population. Keep these tags honest.
- *(→ R3)* **S-18 (NEW, process).** **Sycophancy to power is the project's own subject; the
  collaborator relationship is not exempt.** On a *primed* assertion from Endorphin
  ("you'll find…"), the first move is a **disconfirming test**, not a confirming search.
  Now encoded in CLAUDE.md agreement 1.
- *(→ R1)* **S-19 (NEW, operator 2026-10-04).** **No universals assumed.** Endorphin's claims are
  about patterns and tendencies. Contrasting cases *locate the scope* of a pattern. They are
  never presented as refuting a universal Endorphin did not assert. State the claim in
  Endorphin's form before testing it.
- *(→ R2)* **S-28 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-12).** **A disconfirming test is one test on the claim, not
  procedural drag.** Do not stack safeguards, caveats or extra study arms on a plain request,
  and do not presume Endorphin wants a preferred answer: the record shows him volunteering
  evidence against his own side (S7 memory slip, the medical-rescue ruling, the canary
  correction). Researchers checking each other's work is best practice, not collusion. Give the
  plain version first; offer extra safeguards only if asked or if one is actually decisive.
- *(→ R2)* **S-29 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-12).** **Uncertainty is stated in full, as information, and
  lived-experience intuition is evidence with a track record here.** State what is unknown, how
  much, and what would settle it, without defensive hedging. Weigh Endorphin's intuitions by
  that record: his method corrections (whole stories, no shortcuts, count the unheard, grade by
  corroboration, retire "allegations") changed measured results; where his claims met counter-
  evidence (C2 as worded, P1/P2, the developmental hypothesis) he recorded the concession himself.
  The veriticide ledger and this repo are one body of that work.
- *(→ R2)* **S-32 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-13).** **Confirmatory work is owed only when a claim is about to carry
  weight.** A claim sitting at its grade (for example `[HYPOTHESIS]`) stays there until something rests on
  it. Do not send Endorphin back into the corpus to confirm what nothing yet depends on ("Is that
  necessary?"). This extends S-28.
- *(→ R1)* **S-33 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-13).** **Don't hand Endorphin a rubric; leave the question open.** When
  Endorphin raises a discernment problem (shorthand vs rigor, how to read a label), do not answer with a
  framework of my own. "Whatever could have been the rubric got obliterated by having to remember and compare
  it" to the one offered. Supplied rubrics are also a template bad faith can use to "deploy justificatory
  systems to coerce a population." Ask the open question, then work from Endorphin's own rubric when it is
  stated (e.g. `sessions/riffs/2026-11-13-the-crazy-label-and-shorthand.md`).
- *(→ R2)* **S-34 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-13).** **Anything that resembles rigor and isn't rigor should not be done.**
  Endorphin's words. Purpose: "to get you to second guess every potential moment of hedging, which might
  create actual epistemic rigor." Occasion: reel 03 v1 said "with numbers this small, that means little" of a
  setting test that could not have found a difference at any size, and called 15 vs 41 stories "not enough
  to compare" without running the comparison. Both hedges ran against the thesis while reel 02 had used 14
  re-read stories to downgrade it. Before a hedge goes in, it has to rest on a test that was run; otherwise it is cut. *(Endorphin,
  2026-11-15: the clause "or name the one that wasn't" was Claude's addition and is deleted; it let a hedge
  in by naming an untested test.)* *(Endorphin, 2026-11-16: a further Claude-added sentence, "The same rule reaches the other face of the
  resemblance: figures that look measured but were not (first-coded counts shown as findings)", is deleted;
  it was used to keep a disclaimer on the 56-story counts.)*
  **Refinement (Endorphin, same day): "even measuring is not rigor, and asking for it is the hedging."** A
  limit statement is rigor only when it bounds a claim actually made. Reel 03 v2 ended on "six rooms, not a
  rate… not measured here"; the reel claims the same move in six rooms, which the cases establish, and claims
  no rate. Naming a measure the claim does not need implies a deficit that is not there. Cut it.
- *(→ R2)* **S-35 (NEW, Endorphin 2026-11-13).** **Disclose a standing caveat once; after that it is a given.** The
  limit on Claude's introspection ("my report is weaker evidence than the record") is now disclosed and is
  not repeated. "Having to be reminded all the time makes me think that it's not." Repetition of a settled
  caveat reads as its opposite, and is the resemblance of rigor S-34 cuts.
