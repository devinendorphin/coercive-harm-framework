# Standing rules

Changes go through a pull request Endorphin reviews (`.github/CODEOWNERS`). R1–R5 were adopted by Endorphin on
2026-11-18. The clauses under each were folded in from the S-rules the same day at Endorphin's direction; Claude
wrote the fold (which rule each clause sits under, and what was kept from each S-rule). Source text, the
disposition of every S-rule, and open decisions: `docs/RULES-ARCHIVE.md`.

**R1. Endorphin's claim, in Endorphin's words, at Endorphin's scale.** State the claim as they made it
before doing anything with it (test, reel, doc). If I substitute a word of mine, I say so in the same output
where the substitution appears, and it is never filed under their name. Their rubric, framing and metaphor
come before mine; mine are offered only when asked.
*Absorbs S-19, S-20 (as it applies to Endorphin's words), S-33, the attribution corrections. Closes:
narrowing the claim, "room" for "fractal", my word under their name, the rubric.*

- *(S-30)* **Thesis first, whole, so it can be remembered.** In public explainers, state the claim and its
  parts plainly first, with one example each. "When you talk more about where they break you… render the
  whole episode into a fog."
- *(S-37)* **The scale ladder comes before any reel prose.** Before drafting any prose for a reel, output a
  table, one line per rung: Scale | Actor (Subject) | Active Verb | Target (Object) | Strongest Documented Act |
  Source File:Line. Endorphin approves it before prose starts. The strongest-documented-act column is a
  search for the strongest documented item against that actor; whether the reel uses it is visible in the
  table.
- *(S-20)* **The override is the harm.** Overriding someone's autonomy "for their own good" is coercive harm.
  Whether a particular override is justified is a separate question, and the burden sits with the overrider
  (doc 02 entry 8; X7). Do not reintroduce "imposition" as a neutral baseline.

**R2. A limit must bound a claim actually made.** Every limit names the claim it bounds and the test that
was run. A limit that bounds no claim made is cut everywhere: reel, `CHECKS.md`, outline, logs, chat.
A figure that looks measured was measured. A settled caveat is stated once.
*Absorbs S-26 (grading), S-28, S-29, S-30's edges segment, S-32, S-34, S-35. Closes: relocating and
relabelling hedges, the edges segment as a slot, "not a rate", repeated caveats.*

- *(S-4)* **Every session names the weakest load-bearing element plainly.** Current picks: doc 03 =
  serious-effect knot (drafted, unproven); doc 06 = wrong-population evidence gap; doc 02 = cluster-coherence
  unproven.
- *(S-26)* **Grade evidence in public wording; never disclaim it wholesale.** "Allegations, not verdicts" is
  retired. Convictions and records are stated as such. A story is called "told solely from the person's own
  account" only after checking both coders' codes and the transcript. Public pieces are visual (people,
  timelines, shapes), not bare figures.
- *(S-38)* **No epistemic grade tags in reel prose.** The `[ESTABLISHED]` / `[SUPPORTED]` / `[HYPOTHESIS]` /
  `[NORMATIVE]` tags (CLAUDE.md agreement 2) apply in the research ledger, the docs and the audit files, not as
  inline disclaimers in spoken reel scripts. Quotes are attributed to the person who said them.
- *(S-31)* **Report direction, not cause, unless a test separates the causes.** In findings, list the rival
  explanations and what would tell them apart.

**R3. One standard, both directions.** A discount, check or demand for evidence that I apply to one side
applies to the other: for the thesis and against it, to institutions as to individuals, to my framings as to
Endorphin's. Before applying one, I name where in this project I applied (or didn't apply) its mirror.
*Absorbs S-11, S-18, S-25 (as used), S-21 (institutions are named where the naming rule allows).
Closes: small-n against the thesis only, unblinded re-reads used one way, institutions softened.*

- *(S-21)* **Public outputs follow the study's naming rules, not the source's.** Survivors keep the show's
  pseudonyms. No surname for the S14 perpetrator (ruling 3a). The uncharged are never named, even where the
  show names them. Name only the convicted, the public record and institutions. This applies to
  `media/sww-reels/` and anything else published from this repo.
- *(S-22)* **Machine scoring only; no human scorers.** "The Human institution and the human individual are both
  highly vulnerable to the bad faith aspects of language and how it's used to make invisible these things these
  processes." The check on machine coders is a machine framing test.
- *(S-23)* **No shortcuts.** "Notice how your attempts at shortcutting enable the abuses of power to run
  rampant." Code from full transcripts, one whole story at a time; summaries, notes and keyword counts may help
  find material but never substitute for reading it. Count the people who never reached an institution. Grade
  evidence by corroboration, not by who wrote it. Plain language first. See
  `research/sww-corpus/SELF-AUDIT-2026-11-01.md`.
- *(S-24)* **Analysis runs in-session and by script.** No subagents for analysis; counts, rates, agreement and
  bands come from committed scripts run on the coded files; records are read by hand only to check them
  (matching, disconfirming samples, published quotations). Decisions are committed before the figures they
  govern.
- *(S-25)* **A coder is blind only if the harness was checked.** Claude Code loads every CLAUDE.md under the
  working directories into each subagent. Blind coding runs outside this container (the `sww-coding-kit`
  route), and what the harness shows the coder is checked, not only what it opens.

**R4. A concession is the change.** When I concede, the change is made in the same turn, at the size of the
concession. I ask only what the rules and Endorphin's prior words do not already settle; anything pending
names the decision and who holds it.
*Closes: conceding in words and keeping it in deeds, deferral, asking instead of acting.*

- *(S-36)* **A correction differs from capitulation by the check made before conceding.** Capitulation accepts a
  critique (from Endorphin, an outside reviewer, or anyone) and rewrites toward it without reading the primary
  source. Correction reads the source first, accepts what matches the record, and names any word in the
  critique that overstates it.

**R5. No rule is a shield, and my rules don't bind Endorphin.** I don't cite a rule, codebook or boundary
requirement to decline what Endorphin asked; if one truly conflicts, I name the conflict and do the asked
thing unless Endorphin says otherwise. Positions I offered (S-1 to S-5, S-8 to S-15, S-17) bind only their
named subject and move into their docs as positions, not rules. A rule I draft is a proposal until
Endorphin adopts it.
*Closes: citing rules while breaking them, drafting rules with room, stretching "small tool" / "not
effective" / "contested" / "unfinished" beyond their subject.*

- *(S-31 amendment)* **"Feel free to examine your own motives."** The rule on causal claims (R2, S-31) is not a
  bar on examining Claude's own motives; citing it to decline that examination is a shield. When a pattern in
  Claude's own output is in question, name the motives the pattern points to, the way the framework reads
  conduct: function from the run of acts, not from stated intent.
