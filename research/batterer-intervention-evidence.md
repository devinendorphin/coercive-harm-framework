# Research: Batterer-Intervention Program (BIP) Evidence — the reality floor for doc 06

*Compiled 2026-07-09. The closest real-world analogue to "treatment for those who
harm." This is the honest benchmark doc 06 must absorb now that it is a *primary*
instrument (division of labor). The headline is discouraging — but the detail
partly vindicates doc 06's design choices.*

## Bottom line up front

Mainstream BIPs (decades of them) show **small-to-null effects on recidivism**. That
is a serious reality check on any therapeutic optimism. **But** two details matter for
this framework: (1) the failing programs are overwhelmingly **court-mandated,
confrontational Duluth-model** groups — the *imposed* modality the seed's self-arrival
constraint predicts will fail; and (2) the approaches now **outperforming** Duluth are
**ACT-based and MI-enhanced** — the self-directed, non-confrontational modalities doc 06
already bets on. So the evidence is dismal for the old paradigm and cautiously pointed
toward doc 06's paradigm — without proving it.

## The discouraging headline `[ESTABLISHED]`

- Meta-analyses (Babcock et al. 2004; Feder & Wilson; and a 2024 update, *J. Crim.
  Justice*) find **small** effects. With rigorous experimental designs and non-self-
  report measures, effects fall to **d ≈ 0.09** — Duluth ≈ **5% success increase over
  arrest alone.** Counternull analyses put a *null effect* as roughly as probable as a
  modest one.
- **Duluth ≈ CBT:** intervention type is not a significant moderator; both modest.
- **High attrition** is endemic (dropout is itself a recidivism predictor).
- Victim/couple-report recidivism runs *higher* than official-report — i.e., official
  numbers **understate** reoffending, so the true picture is if anything worse.

## The detail that matters for doc 06 `[SUPPORTED]`

- **The failing modality is the *imposed* one.** BIPs are overwhelmingly court-mandated
  and confrontational (Duluth). That mandated/confrontational condition is *exactly*
  what the seed's **self-arrival constraint** and psychological-reactance predict will
  underperform (doc 06 §1.4, §2). So the low Duluth numbers are partly **evidence *for*
  the self-arrival premise**, not simply against the therapeutic enterprise.
- **The improving modalities are doc 06's.** "Other" interventions — **ACT-based**
  (Zarling's **ACTV**, Iowa State; **Circles of Peace**) — now **outperform Duluth.**
  ACTV showed lower recidivism (~9%) but **not statistically significant** (COVID cut
  the sample) — *promising, not proven.*
- **MI has direct (if early) BIP support.** Motivational strategies are "a promising way
  to... enhance effectiveness," with 2025 evidence that MI improves **adherence and
  reduces recidivism**, especially in perpetrators with substance-use comorbidity
  (Frontiers in Psychiatry 2025; systematic review PMC10486163). This gives doc 06's
  **MI operational core** a *direct* toehold in the perpetrator population — better than
  the addiction-only analogue we had.

## The honest limits (do not overclaim)

1. **Effects are small even for the better programs**, and often non-significant.
2. **Outcome = physical-violence recidivism**, measured by re-arrest — **not** the
   coercive-control / *psychological* pattern doc 06 centers. Essentially **no**
   evidence that any program remediates the standalone words-only/coercive pattern.
3. **Mandation paradox unresolved.** BIP evidence is on *mandated* populations; doc 06's
   self-arrival logic wants *self-originated* change. The promising MI/ACT signals are
   still delivered inside mandates, so "self-directed inside a mandate" remains the
   unresolved design tension (doc 06 open question).

## Implications for doc 06

- **Set an honest evidence floor:** the framework must NOT promise rehabilitation.
  Mainstream "treatment for those who harm" barely beats arrest alone. Doc 06 is
  aspirational with a discouraging base rate.
- **The design is pointed the right way:** ACT/MI (self-directed, values-based, non-
  confrontational) outperform Duluth (imposed, confrontational) — consistent with the
  self-arrival constraint. Keep MI as the operational core (now with direct BIP
  support), but tag efficacy-for-this-population `[HYPOTHESIS]`.
- **Reframes OBJ-005/OBJ-012:** policy's deliverable is a *better-designed, self-
  directed, MI/ACT-informed* pathway — not a promise it works. The claim is
  "least-bad and correctly oriented," not "effective."
