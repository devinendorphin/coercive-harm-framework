# Research: Coercion Continuity Across Scale ("fascism is interpersonal violence at scale")

*Compiled 2026-07-09 for `/develop 03`, at Endorphin's direction. Tests the claim
that the coercion mechanisms of interpersonal abuse are the same ones deployed by
authoritarian/fascist political power — i.e., whether the geopolitical rung of the
cross-scale claim (doc 01) is evidentially load-bearing rather than ornamental.*

## Verdict up front

The claim is **substantially true and well-supported at the level of mechanism and
of causal continuity.** It is NOT fringe — it rests on foundational, widely-cited
work (Herman; Biderman via Amnesty; Stark) and on a large recent empirical
literature (DV→mass-violence). It becomes overclaim only in its strict *reductive-
identity* reading (fascism = nothing but interpersonal abuse scaled up), which
ignores fascism's structural/economic/mobilizational dimensions. Claim the mechanism
and the continuity; don't claim the reduction. **The geopolitical rung is therefore
evidentially load-bearing — my earlier "ornamental" call was wrong.**

## The three readings of "is" (assessed separately)

### (a) Mechanism homology — STRONGLY SUPPORTED `[ESTABLISHED]`
"Fascism/political terror uses the *same coercion mechanisms* as interpersonal
abuse, scaled up."

- **Biderman's Chart of Coercion** (1957): eight methods — isolation,
  monopolization of perception, humiliation/degradation, exhaustion, threats,
  occasional indulgences, demonstrating omnipotence, enforcing trivial demands —
  derived from **Korean War POW torture** (a *state* deploying coercion). **Amnesty
  International (1973)** called these "the universal tools of torture and coercion."
  The *same* chart was then transferred to **domestic violence** by Ann Jones, Susan
  Schechter, and **Evan Stark** (the coercive-control scholar). One taxonomy, two
  scales — the cross-scale validation was done independently, decades ago, in
  institutional and scholarly contexts. [ESTABLISHED]
- These eight methods map almost directly onto doc 02's seed-derived taxonomy
  (isolation → "separate you from family and friends," L16; monopolization of
  perception → "deceptive atmosphere," L59; demonstrating omnipotence + trivial
  demands → "seeds of chaos," L80). **This is external, validated grounding for the
  taxonomy** — a partial answer to OBJ-001 (confirmation bias).

### (b) Causal / developmental continuity — SUPPORTED `[SUPPORTED]`
"Interpersonal coercive violence feeds, predicts, and enables mass/political
violence."

- **Judith Herman, *Trauma and Recovery: The Aftermath of Violence — From Domestic
  Abuse to Political Terror*** (1992): the entire thesis is that trauma from private
  terror (battering, incest) and public terror (combat, political captivity) operates
  by the same mechanisms and must be understood in a shared social/political frame.
  Foundational, mainstream. [ESTABLISHED as scholarship; the continuity thesis
  SUPPORTED]
- **DV → mass-violence pipeline (empirical):** ~**60% of mass shooters** have a
  documented DV/IPV history; **59.1% of 2014–2019 mass shootings were DV-related**;
  misogyny/gender-based grievance is "the biggest and clearest predictor of mass
  shootings, across ideologies." "Intimate terrorism" (total control of a partner)
  and the mass-shooter control pattern are described as the same drive. (Everytown;
  The Trace; PMC 2021 study.) This is the hard empirical bridge from the interpersonal
  to the mass/political. [ESTABLISHED for the correlation; causal reading SUPPORTED]

### (c) Reductive identity — OVERCLAIM, do not assert
"Fascism *is nothing but* interpersonal abuse scaled up / reduces to it."

- **Jason Stanley, *How Fascism Works*** (2018) actually supports the *template*
  version, not the reduction: fascist politics is organized around the **patriarchal
  family** — "the leader of the nation is analogous to the father in the traditional
  patriarchal family" — with ten pillars (mythic past, propaganda, anti-
  intellectualism, **unreality**, **hierarchy**, **victimhood**, law-and-order,
  **sexual anxiety**, heartland, dismantling public goods). Note the overlaps with
  the seed: *unreality* = truth-inversion/deceptive atmosphere; *victimhood* =
  confession-through-accusation (DARVO) at national scale.
- But fascism also has structural-economic, bureaucratic, industrial-mobilization,
  and mass-party dimensions that are **not** reducible to a bad partner writ large.
  The homology is about the **control mechanism**, not the whole phenomenon. Claiming
  (c) would be the kind of overreach a critic uses to dismiss the sound (a)/(b) core.

## The hazard the framework must guard against (behavior-not-people, applied here)

The claim is safe **in the forward direction** — "authoritarian/fascist power
deploys coercion tactics also documented in interpersonal abuse" is a statement about
*tactics/behavior*. It becomes dangerous **run backward** — "individuals who show
these behaviors are proto-fascists / a fascist *type* / have the fascist brain."
That reverse inference is exactly the person-typing docs 02 and 07 forbid, and it is
the specific way this otherwise-strong claim could corrupt the taxonomy. **Keep the
arrow pointing from mechanism→scale, never from individual→political-identity.**
The literature *helps* here: Biderman, Herman, and Stanley are all explicitly about
tactics and structures, not personality types.
