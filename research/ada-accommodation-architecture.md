# Research: ADA Accommodation Architecture (for doc 05)

*Compiled 2026-07-09 for `/develop 05`. Tests whether the ADA can carry the
accommodation model and how to resolve OBJ-004 (disability framing = person-typing).
Sources: EEOC enforcement guidance; JAN; 7th Cir. reassignment case law.*

## Bottom line up front

The ADA **already separates status from conduct** in exactly the way the framework
needs — but using the ADA as a *legal entitlement* requires a diagnosed "qualified
individual with a disability," which re-triggers the person-typing OBJ-004 forbids.
**Resolution: borrow the ADA's *architecture* (status/conduct split + prospective
structural accommodation + reassignment-as-accommodation), applied on the basis of
*documented conduct*, without invoking the ADA's *legal disability classification*.**
The cost is enforceability — see the weakest-element note in doc 05.

## The four doctrines that matter

1. **Status ≠ conduct; conduct standards bind everyone.** EEOC: "improper behavior in
   and of itself does not constitute a disability, and having a disability does not
   excuse employees from... following the same conduct standards required of all
   employees." Employers may enforce job-related conduct rules even when a disability
   is involved. [ESTABLISHED]
2. **No duty to excuse *past* misconduct.** "Because reasonable accommodation is
   always prospective, an employer is not required to excuse past misconduct" — nor
   rescind discipline (up to termination) warranted by it. [ESTABLISHED]
3. **Accommodation is *prospective and structural*.** "An employer must make
   reasonable accommodation to enable an otherwise qualified individual... to meet
   conduct standards *in the future*, barring undue hardship." If misconduct is
   disability-related, the ADA may require a forward-looking accommodation to prevent
   *future* misconduct. [ESTABLISHED]
4. **Reassignment / demotion as accommodation ("last resort").** When no accommodation
   lets the employee perform the essential functions of the current role, reassignment
   to a vacant position — including, if no equivalent exists, a **vacant lower-level
   position (demotion)** — is a recognized accommodation. Pay need not be maintained.
   Requires a "qualified individual with a disability." [ESTABLISHED]

## Why this maps onto the seed's festival-director example (L118)

Endorphin's prescription — "outreach [role]... not be given any financial strings...
no one under her" — is **doctrinally a prospective structural accommodation**: it
removes the leverage (budget authority, subordinates) through which the documented
harm occurs, enabling the person to meet the conduct standard ("do not harm those
under your authority") going forward, by removing the authority. It is the framework's
own instance of reassignment/role-design-as-accommodation. The seed reached the ADA's
architecture intuitively.

## The OBJ-004 resolution (status/conduct split does the work)

- **The person-typing trap:** to claim ADA accommodation *as a right*, you must
  establish a "qualified individual with a **disability**" — a diagnosis. That is the
  classification of a *person* by *condition*, which docs 02/07 forbid and OBJ-004
  attacks.
- **The escape:** don't claim the legal disability status. Adopt the ADA's *conduct-
  based architecture* as a **voluntary organizational-design practice**:
  1. conduct standards bind everyone equally (this is the behavior-not-people spine,
     and it is *also* what protects potential subordinates);
  2. no excusing of past harm (protects victims; not a "sick note");
  3. accommodation = **forward-looking role design keyed to documented conduct and
     risk**, not to a diagnosed type;
  4. reassignment/demotion precedent legitimizes "no budget / no reports" as a
     recognized, non-punitive employment move rather than a scarlet letter.
- Net: **no one is classified as disabled; the organization applies conduct-based,
  ADA-shaped role design.** The word "disability" survives only as the *design
  analogy* Endorphin explicitly flagged as a "framing" (L118) — not a clinical or
  legal label. OBJ-004 dissolves; OBJ-012 (two-tier person-typing) is answered by
  sorting on documented conduct + risk, never inferred capacity-to-change.

## The costs (honest)

- **Enforceability.** Strip the legal disability hook and you lose the legal *duty*
  to accommodate — the model becomes voluntary best-practice org design, exactly what
  the seed conceded doesn't exist yet ("no organization has [the] capacity to
  accommodate," L118). Aspirational culture, not an enforceable right. **This is doc
  05's weakest load-bearing element.**
- **Adverse-action exposure.** Role-stripping based on documented conduct, absent
  either a disability process or a disciplinary finding, can itself be challenged as
  wrongful demotion / defamation / discrimination if the conduct read is biased or
  pretextual. The assessment must be documented, legitimate, non-pretextual — and this
  is a both-victims pressure point (a biased "conduct read" is a weaponization vector).
