# Research: Therapeutic-Modality Evidence (for doc 06)

*Compiled 2026-07-09 for `/develop 06`. Tests the seed's therapeutic pathways
(mindfulness/Siegel, IFS, self-arrival constraint) against current evidence. The
steelman of the offense draft (OBJ-023) just made doc 06 a **primary** instrument for
the pure-words case criminal law can't reach — raising the stakes on this evidence.*

## Bottom line up front

Each modality has real evidence — **but almost entirely for trauma *survivors* and
general stressed populations, not for the people who *enact* the coercive pattern.**
The seed's therapeutic pathway assumes these tools help the *person with the deficit*
develop missing capacities; that transfer is **unvalidated** and is doc 06's weakest
element. The one genuinely new lever the seed lacked is **Motivational Interviewing**,
which is the evidence-based method for eliciting *self-directed* change in
low-insight/resistant people — a partial answer to OBJ-005.

## Finding 1 — Mindfulness → structural brain change `[ESTABLISHED]` (but mind the population)

- **Hölzel & Lazar (2011):** 8-week MBSR produced measurable **gray-matter changes**
  (hippocampus and other regions) vs. controls — the landmark support for the seed's
  Siegel-derived claim that practice can build "the actual structures." Faster still:
  RCTs find white-matter changes around ACC/PCC in **2–4 weeks / 5–10 hours** (Tang et
  al.). Neuroplasticity is real and relatively fast. [ESTABLISHED]
- **Two honest caveats the seed's version overstates:**
  1. **Population.** These studies are on ordinary/stressed volunteers improving
     attention and emotion regulation — **not** on people lacking empathy/theory-of-mind
     remediating a coercive-behavior deficit. The seed's "enables these capacities for
     the first time" in the deficit population is `[HYPOTHESIS]`, not shown.
  2. **Region + timeline.** The seed emphasizes **mPFC** and "a decade." The robust
     findings are gray-matter density changes in **8 weeks** in several regions; the
     specific mPFC-remediation-of-the-cluster claim is unproven, and the "decade"
     figure is Endorphin's estimate, not a research finding. (Ties to OBJ-002.)

## Finding 2 — Internal Family Systems (IFS) `[SUPPORTED]`, emerging

- IFS moved from "evidence-informed" to early RCT-level support: the **first RCT of
  PARTS** (Program for Alleviating and Resolving Trauma and Stress, a 16-week
  IFS-derived group protocol), Cambridge Health Alliance / Harvard, published in
  *Psychological Trauma* (APA). Proof-of-concept + pilot data show good acceptability
  and PTSD-symptom reduction (PCL-5). [SUPPORTED — emerging, small-N, early]
- **Same caveat, sharper:** the evidence is for **PTSD in trauma survivors** — i.e., the
  framework's *victims*, not the coercive-pattern population. Using IFS-for-victims to
  support IFS-for-the-deficit-population is a category transfer with **no** direct
  evidence. [HYPOTHESIS for the target population]

## Finding 3 — Reactance + Motivational Interviewing `[ESTABLISHED]` (the missing lever)

- **Psychological reactance** — resistance to perceived pressure — is well-established
  and vindicates the seed's **self-arrival constraint** ("if anyone they have beef with
  says 'here's a solution,' the last thing they'll do is take it," L118). Externally
  imposed change increases defensiveness; **self-generated** motivation produces more
  durable change (distinct neural pathways). [ESTABLISHED]
- **Motivational Interviewing (MI)** is the evidence-based clinical method built for
  exactly this: it reduces reactance by having the person **articulate their own
  dissonance** rather than being argued at; directive confrontation is *iatrogenic* for
  high-resistance people, non-directive works. MI was developed in addiction — a
  denial/resistance-dominant field — which is the closest analogue to the low-insight
  coercive-pattern population. [ESTABLISHED for addiction/health behavior]
- **Why this matters:** MI is a **trainable, deployable** stance that honors "the person
  must arrive at it themselves" *without* leaving policy with nothing to do. It is the
  bridge the seed's framework was missing between the self-arrival constraint and
  OBJ-005 ("a self-directed-only cure gives policy nothing to do"). Still `[HYPOTHESIS]`
  for *this* population specifically (MI's evidence is addiction/health, not
  coercive-control perpetration).

## Implications for doc 06

1. **State the population gap plainly** — it is the weakest load-bearing element: the
   modalities are evidence-based for *victims/general populations*, and their transfer
   to the *coercive-pattern population* is unvalidated. Given the steelman's
   division-of-labor finding (doc 06 is now primary for the words-only case), this gap
   sits at the framework's center.
2. **Promote MI to the operational core** — it converts "self-directed only" from a
   dead end into a method, and partially answers OBJ-005.
3. **Keep mindfulness/IFS as `[SUPPORTED]`-for-wellbeing, `[HYPOTHESIS]`-for-remediation**;
   do not let the strong victim-side evidence launder the unproven perpetrator-side claim
   (tag-drift risk, the /evidence-check failure mode).
4. **Jessa Reed / personal-mythology** stays `[HYPOTHESIS]`, n=1, illustrative.
