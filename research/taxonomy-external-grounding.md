# Research: External Grounding for the Behavioral Taxonomy (doc 02)

*Compiled 2026-07-09 for `/develop 02`. Purpose: replace "one man's pattern-matching"
(OBJ-001) with validated external constructs, and operationalize each seed term as
observable conduct rather than an inferred trait.*

## The move

The seed's taxonomy was coined from lived experience. To survive the empirical-
psychologist critique (OBJ-001), each term should be **mapped onto an existing,
researched construct** and **restated as third-party-observable conduct**. Three
external anchors: Biderman (already in doc 02), Stark's coercive control, and — the new
one — **DARVO**.

## DARVO — validates "confession through accusation" `[SUPPORTED]`

- **DARVO** = **Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim and Offender.** Coined by **Jennifer Freyd
  (1997**, *Feminism & Psychology*). The perpetrator denies the conduct, attacks the
  person confronting them, and **reverses victim/offender roles** — casting themselves
  as the victim and the true victim as the offender.
- **This is precisely the seed's "confession through accusation"** (L80): "when a
  problematic personality is accusing you of something [it] is generally of the very
  thing that they are doing." DARVO is the named, researched version.
- **Empirical status:** emerging but real. Harsey, Zurbriggen & Freyd (2017) — a
  confrontation study found **~72% of perpetrators used DARVO**; exposure to DARVO made
  observers rate the victim as **less believable and more responsible** and the
  perpetrator as **less abusive**, and increased victims' **self-blame**. Harsey & Freyd
  (2020, 2023) extend to credibility perceptions. [SUPPORTED — construct validated,
  effects replicated; base-rate figure from a single confrontation sample]
- **Double payoff:** DARVO's "reverse victim and offender" *is* the weaponization-
  inversion doc 07 guards against — an abuser claiming victim status. So grounding the
  taxonomy in DARVO simultaneously grounds the both-victims safeguard. It also warns:
  any reporting/adjudication mechanism will encounter DARVO *at the point of
  complaint*, which is an argument for the predominant-aggressor screen (doc 03 §9 §5).

## The operationalization discipline (conduct, not mind-state)

The most person-typing-prone term is **"performed empathy masking absent theory-of-
mind"** — it asserts an *internal state* (they lack the capacity but fake it). For a
conduct-based framework this must be **restated observably**, e.g.: "displays of
understanding that are **contradicted by subsequent conduct** — stated attunement not
borne out in action, over a documented pattern." That is third-party-checkable and
carries no diagnosis. Same test applied to every term: *what would a witness see?*

## The honest limit (OBJ-001 residue)

External grounding ≠ a validated *instrument for the cluster as a whole*. Biderman,
Stark, DARVO each validate *components*; that the components **co-occur as a single
coherent "cluster"** in identifiable people is still `[HYPOTHESIS]` and is the part most
exposed to confirmation bias. Doc 02 should claim the validated components and flag the
cluster-coherence claim as unproven — do not let component validation launder the
whole-cluster claim (tag drift).
