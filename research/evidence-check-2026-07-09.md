# Evidence-Check Scorecard — full framework, 2026-07-09

*`/evidence-check all`. Audits empirical claims across docs 00–08 for citation and tag
drift. Two material corrections and several confirmations. The mindfulness correction
is the most important finding and changes a doc-06 mechanism claim.*

## Material corrections (tags changed)

### DOWNGRADE — mindfulness → structural brain change: `[ESTABLISHED]` → `[HYPOTHESIS / CONTESTED]`
- **Was:** doc 06 §1 and `research/therapeutic-modalities-evidence.md` cited Hölzel/Lazar
  (2011) 8-week gray-matter change as `[ESTABLISHED]`, grounding the seed's "practice
  builds the actual structures" premise (Siegel, L133).
- **New evidence:** **Kral, Davidson et al. (2022, *Science Advances*)** — the largest,
  most rigorously controlled study (N=218, two 3-arm RCTs) — **failed to replicate** any
  MBSR-induced structural change, at whole-brain or ROI level, vs. controls. The earlier
  small studies (cited 3,200+ times) do not hold up.
- **Correction:** the *structural-change* claim is now **contested and probably not
  established**; downgrade to `[HYPOTHESIS]`. Mindfulness's benefits for *attention,
  stress, wellbeing* remain separately supported — but the specific "grows/builds brain
  structure in 8 weeks" mechanism, which is what the seed's therapeutic premise leans on,
  should NOT be asserted. This weakens doc 06's mechanism and reinforces its
  weakest-element note. (Kral et al. speculate longer/other practice *might* do it —
  unproven.)

### UPGRADE + CITE — coercive control → serious psychological harm: now `[ESTABLISHED]`, cited
- **Was:** doc 01 asserted coercive control's psychological harm as `[SUPPORTED]`/
  `[ESTABLISHED]` but **uncited**.
- **New evidence:** systematic review + meta-analysis (Monash, 2023, *Trauma, Violence &
  Abuse*): coercive control is **moderately associated with PTSD (r = .32) and depression
  (r = .27)** — comparable in strength to *physical* IPV; "psychological violence should
  be acknowledged as a severe form of IPV equal to physical and sexual violence."
- **Correction:** confirm `[ESTABLISHED]` for coercive control → serious **psychological**
  harm, now cited. **But keep the boundary:** the seed's stronger "concrete physical
  effect on the **body**" / literal bodily-injury framing (L16) is *not* what this
  supports — that remains `[HYPOTHESIS]`. Psychological/mental-health harm: established.
  Literal physical injury from words: unproven.
- **Bonus — partially answers OBJ-019:** the foreseeability of *psychological* harm from
  the coercive pattern has **meta-analytic** support (r = .32 with PTSD), independent of
  the physical-lethality instruments (Danger Assessment). So the foreseeability keystone
  (doc 04 §0) is better-supported for psychological harm than OBJ-019 implied — though
  r = .32 is a *moderate* association, not a deterministic predictor. Refine, don't
  overclaim.
