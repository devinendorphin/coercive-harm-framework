# Identity decisions, 2026-11-11 (before headline calculations)

Matching used identity-only exports. `matching_hand.csv` records every manual one-to-one
match and unmatched disposition for the neutral pairs and all four S17 cross-family pairs.
Automatic matches preserve parenthetical harm labels and require unique full normalised
names. Names and corresponding perpetrator roles were reviewed for automatic matches;
remaining identities were compared using harm descriptions. Codes were not consulted.

No person is matched to two records in one comparison. Where coders combine different
perpetrators/programs or groups, those records remain unmatched. Unnamed children and dancers
without distinguishing details are not assigned to one another by arbitrary numbering.
This makes person-finding agreement conservative; disagreements include differences in how
stories are divided, not just omissions.

`repeat_links.csv` records clear cross-season continuations; within-season matches propagate
identity links to the other coder. The analysis retains the earliest record within each
reporting group and each own-coder stream, before examining eligibility or agreement.
A record that exists only for one coder still contributes to that coder’s own analysis.
Separate harms remain separate. The unchanged season-based result is primary.

Ambiguities are retained separately: the London doula called Amy in S19 cannot be identified
as either of the two different Amys in S18 from the permitted identity fields; Jubilee’s
S23 prophecy/cover-up harm may overlap S21 religious harm, but the named institutions and
perpetrator roles are not sufficiently specific to merge them; the host’s unspecified toxic
employers in S23 cannot be equated with the pregnancy dismissal in S16. The grouped S24 doula
update is not one identified S18 individual. Repeated first names with different harms or
perpetrators are not links. The deduplicated figures therefore mean identifiable
person–harm stories, not an exact count of unique human beings.
