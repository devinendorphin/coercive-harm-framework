## What this does to the verdicts

Both lexicon-based predictions (P1, P2) were refuted on the pre-registered test. The audit
does not rescue them and does not deepen the refutation: an indicator with 25–50 % precision
measures the construct weakly in every season, so a null difference between power positions
is what a weak instrument would also produce. **The pre-registered verdicts stand as
"refuted"; the audit is reported beside them.** This is the same treatment isolation (about
55 %) and reversal (about 80 %) received.
