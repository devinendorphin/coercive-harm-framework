## Divergences

1. **The apex asymmetry in one family.** The private actor's coercive-exploitative conduct was
   reached by criminal law; the state actor's killing of his son only by a civil payment.
   Reachability by instrument, not equivalence of conduct; one case; the criminal disposition
   is U, not "none."
2. **The coached interview** is the child-scale form of forced proximity in process; the NICHD
   protocol remedy is **symmetric by design** (protects an accused parent too).
3. **Pattern evidence did corroborative work** in a published appellate holding, for
   transaction-documented conduct; it does not reach the words-only case.
4. **Nominee fronts defeat role restriction** (doc 05's instrument) where the restricted
   person has family to front for them.
5. **Diagnostic labels given to a child** inside an adversarial family (walked through ASPD
   criteria about her father as a teenager): the person-typing doc 07 guards against, as a
   harm in its own right. The framework adopts none of the family's labels.
6. **A words-only effect, observed from outside**: she removed her own voice from the show
   in 2021–23 after the mother's post, and listeners asked where it had gone. Causation is
   `[HYPOTHESIS]` (confounds she names herself).
