## Divergences

1. **The verification loop is the mechanism.** Every check a target could make ran through
   the operator. This is fabricated third parties at their limit, and it defeats the
   codebook's usual sign that the network can correct the target: here the network *was*
   the perpetrator.
2. **No statute fit the harm.** Sex by deception as to identity, years of fabricated medical
   crises and covert surveillance produced one misdemeanour, dismissed. Law's categories,
   not the evidence, ended the case.
3. **The complainants' choice to dismiss** for a felony that never came is a cost of the
   gap, borne by them.
4. **Both-victims record (doc 07).** The show named her, read Hare's psychopathy checklist
   over her (Agreement 3: not adopted), repeated an uncorroborated claim of hers to the
   official it concerned, and organised a petition; she lost a hospital job and faced
   licence complaints, **all before any adjudication**. The evidence is strong; the
   punishment is extra-legal. Both are true.
5. **X7 inverted.** Every parent and friend who suspected the truth declined to override the
   victim; the only override was the operator's, turning a victim's distress into a
   diagnosis.
6. **A female perpetrator of a romance cycle** (compare S15c, S18b): the move-set does not
   depend on the operator's gender.
