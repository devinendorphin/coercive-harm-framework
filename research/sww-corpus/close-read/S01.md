## Similarities beyond the cycle

- **Fabricated third parties** run every stage. The sockpuppets "Kimmy and Brian" love-bomb
  (letters to "his future wife"), set the rules ("never a sharp tongue", "don't you dare
  ever give up on him"), devalue (weight), isolate (the family is "codependent"), and
  threaten ("Brian had to walk Bubba off a ledge"). He ordered the "gift from Kimmy" from
  the real couple's shop on **2 Oct 2017, 18 days after meeting Sarah** (E10). That is
  early planning.
  - **The same device appears in a different perpetrator.** The host's father, writing
    from prison, sent her a typed letter from a fake "friend of your father" asking for
    money (E12).
- **A threat of suicide via a third party, used as a leash:** Dick (through "Brian") and
  the teenage girl in the voicemail ("FaceTime me in the shower so I don't hurt myself").
  This is the same function across gender, age and sexual orientation.
- **Control after the relationship ends** through technology: Gmail logins, webcam hacking,
  card misuse, demands for mailing addresses "for legal purposes", watching her stories
  from a second account (E9, E10).
- **His family runs the redemption script.** His mother's mass email (ex-fiancée); his
  brother, a church elder, arguing for a marriage "redeemed" after a wife was groomed and
  raped by her father-in-law, and asking whether Dick "was trying to better your
  relationship through a sinful method" (E9).
- **Witnesses are chilled.** "Everyone is literally too afraid" to go on record, for fear of
  hacking (E10). This bears on the documentation burden: the record is suppressed before
  it is made.

## Divergences (recorded, not smoothed)

1. **C5 runs in reverse in the central case.** Sarah exits; she is not discarded. The
   Southwest girlfriend also ends it. "Discard" belongs to his pattern with the ex-fiancée
   and Lindsay 2, not to every case.
2. **The method varies; the function holds.** Sarah: "with her [the ex] he said mean and
   cruel things to her face. With me he learned… let's come up with fake people" (E12).
   Same perpetrator, different instruments.
3. **Sexual coercion appears with Lindsay 2** (pressure, nude photos). It is described as
   absent with Sarah.
4. **Animal cruelty** (kicking Sarah's dog; "playfully" smothering his own dog) is not in
   the operator's six stages. It sits under Duluth's "threats / harming pets" (E3).
5. **Susceptibility differs.** Two women with earlier abusive partners (Southwest, Lindsay
   2) saw it sooner and set limits. The women's histories diverge, and so does how far
   the pattern ran.
6. **The care system acted, and also first silenced itself.** Sarah's sister, mother,
   father and boss's wife each suppressed their doubts to keep the peace. Then they
   compared notes and stopped the wedding (E1, E6). In the ex-fiancée's case her father
   stopped it. In both cases a parent was the circuit-breaker.
7. **Diagnostic framing is the show's, not a finding.** The host reads DSM/ASPD and
   *Psychopath Free* material (E3, E9, E10). This framework codes conduct, not diagnoses.

## What this season establishes for the operator's claim

- **Strong, within one perpetrator.** Six women who did not know each other describe the
  same opening script (sometimes literally the same text), the same binding devices
  (cancer, church attacks, book replacement) and the same post-relationship pursuit. The
  details that differ are, as the operator predicted, largely **name, place and time**,
  with the divergences listed above.
- **Suggestive, across perpetrators**, from only three data points inside S1:
  - the host's father's fake-friend letter;
  - the teenage girl's suicide-threat isolation;
  - Lindsay 2's ex-husband ("almost identical").

  The cross-perpetrator test is what the remaining 25 seasons are for.
- **The vocabulary point:** the host cites Bancroft (*Why Does He Do That?*), the NIH /
  coercive-control sign list, de Becker and *Psychopath Free* on air. The terms come from
  the literature and the clinical-popular canon, as the operator said, not from the show.
