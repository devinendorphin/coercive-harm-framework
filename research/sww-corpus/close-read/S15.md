## E12: the Consent Awareness Network, claims checked (amendment 3; doc 03 §4)

**A correction for the operator's framing after S14.** The operator described the reform
movement as wanting "a Nuremberg definition of consent being the **enthusiastic consent** that
possesses no force, fraud, fear or incapacitation."
- **CAN explicitly rejects** "enthusiastic consent," "affirmative consent," "yes means yes" and
  "no means no" as "victim blaming rhetoric." Its objection is that all four look at the victim's
  words or conduct.
- CAN's standard is **influence-based**: consent is "freely given, knowledgeable and informed
  agreement by a person with the capacity to reason." Consent is vitiated by the offender's
  "force, fear, or fraud" or exploited "incapacity," "no matter what they say or what they do,"
  and the offender's knowledge supplies the intent.
- So **the Nuremberg-style standard and the enthusiastic standard are rival programmes, not one
  programme.** The operator's two other points stand: consent law is a patchwork, and the fraud
  prong is non-intuitive.
