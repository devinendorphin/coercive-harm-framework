## Divergences

1. **Every door pointed to another door.** This is inaction as structure, not as a choice by
   any one office. The power-vector test (2026-11-01) found appropriate responses dominate in
   the corpus's hand-coded segments; S23 is the season where that finding is least true, and
   it should be cited as a case, not as a rate.
2. **The push was real.** Hospital mistreatment (unconsented genetic tests; CDC VitalSigns
   2023, about 1 in 5 mothers mistreated) drove clients here. The harm-laundering runs on a
   legitimate grievance.
3. **The survivors argue process, not outcome** ("bad outcomes ≠ bad midwives"), seek
   disclosure rather than prohibition, and decline to let the student's fault absorb the
   owners'. One husband wants revocation and prosecution; recorded, not adopted.
4. **X7 calibrated.** "You as a provider should have balls to… say this is what we're
   concerned about." She wanted to be **told and referred, not overridden.**
5. **Comparators:** a Miami practice giving misoprostol covertly "as herbal tea"; a South
   Carolina death ruled homicide by a coroner's jury, no charges, licence surrendered (record).
