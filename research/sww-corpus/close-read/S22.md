## Divergences

1. **The hard both-victims case.** She privately described an internal-massage session she did
   not remember consenting to; a group member forwarded it to the practitioner as part of her
   false-accusation pattern. **Propensity is not proof of this event.** Doc 07 must not let a
   record of fabrication become automatic disbelief (compare S18 divergence 1, where police
   disbelief of a serial false reporter was right).
2. **The crowd used the perpetrator's moves**: deception, coercion of a detained person,
   public humiliation, an aim to punish what is not a crime. Behaviour, not people: the same
   fields code the group.
3. **The crowd also did what the state did not** (pooling, interruption). E13's fraud victims,
   with no group, got a pamphlet. Both are true; doc 07's speech and due-process prongs have
   to hold both.
4. **The detection worked on conduct, not on a class of reports.** The case shows a false
   accuser can be identified without any presumption against accusers.
