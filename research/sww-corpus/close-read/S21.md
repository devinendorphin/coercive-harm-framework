## Divergences

1. **A false allegation disproved by records, and the target paid anyway (S21a).** Key-card
   and duty-hour records refuted it; she lost the mentor relationship. X3 needs both halves:
   the record worked, the harm still landed.
2. **"No marks" as the test, three times (S21b, S21c).** A judge, a CPS screener, and the
   survivor's own later employer. The harms chosen were the ones that leave none (S21b).
3. **An acquittal after a medical finding of repeated rape (S21c).** Recorded at full weight;
   the CPS substantiation survives the expunged criminal record. The framework's step-one
   rule says the case file asks for inquiry, not a verdict, and this is the case where the
   verdict and the medical record point opposite ways.
4. **The institution did the binding (S21e).** A "prophet" arranged the marriage from her
   Facebook; leadership had a prior report and demoted the reporter. Compare S5 and S19c.
5. **A protective institution expelled the target (S21d).** The rehab, fed by his paid
   informants, called her a security risk. Reversal routed through a helping institution.
6. **The law had no name for the deepfakes (S21f).** Adult image abuse made with a consumer
   app was not reachable in Michigan in 2023.
7. **Override, both ways (S21e).** The pastor's advice to postpone the wedding made her dig
   in; her mother's "you don't need to make any decisions right now" and a hotline that did
   not pressure her got her out. X7's override test, observed.
8. **The show endorses the act it condemns (E19).** A guest podcaster catfished an uncharged
   suspect under a false identity, praised on air: the S20 conduct, judged by who did it.
