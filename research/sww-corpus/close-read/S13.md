   detail… the exact same with me, except for the physicality… she was 18, so he altered his
   methods for her." The function was constant (role access, secret phone, "if things were
   different," secrecy, discard at exposure). **The form changed at the line where the law
   changes.** This is the corpus's clearest within-perpetrator test, and it shows the
   perpetrator controlling variance, not lacking it.
2. **C3 n/d in two marriages (S13a, S13b).** In both the network was physically present (S13b:
   her sister lived in the house). The control ran through sex, money and the church (S13b),
   and through addiction and the household economy (S13a). Recorded as n/d, not inferred.
3. **C3 attempted and failed in effect (S13h).** Ryan forbade jobs and friends and extracted Zoe
   from a dorm, and her friends stayed ("not one of them did that"). As in S12a, the network
   was protective.
4. **Rubric 2c: three in-scope instances; one question undetermined.**
   - *For:* S13h (Ryan to Zoe about Nora, while Nora's screenshots were genuine); S13n (Jim to
     four women about the others, including Arya); S13o (Brent to his family about Gianna,
     reported by Gianna).
   - *Searched for a counter-instance* (targets inside the system speaking fluently against
     their partner to third parties): **none.** Every target inside defended or explained
     (see T-fields).
   - *The live-in partner (S13n): U.* Arya found an extensive public record (theft, battery,
     the stabbing of an ex-boyfriend), with news coverage. Whether Jim's account of her was
     true is **undetermined, by the operator's ruling** (CODEBOOK 2c). The limit Claude
     proposed is not adopted.
   - *A boundary of form (S13h).* Nora, herself strung along, was fluent and cruel to Zoe
     ("everyone would be happier if you just killed yourself"). That was aimed at a rival, not
     said about her partner, so it is **outside 2c's form**. It is recorded so the form, "about
     their partner," stays explicit.
5. **R-SEP in all nine; one ~.** S13l is a fifth near-class-3 exit: one blaming text, then
   silence. Unlike S11c, S11g, S11h and S12a, his opportunity was cut by the institution.
6. **Pursuit after rejection with no relationship (S13j).** With S11e, a second R-SEP-type
   response without a control sequence. It reinforces the S11 finding that **neither C1 nor
   R-SEP discriminates alone**.
7. **Deception class plus pre-emptive C4 (S13k).** S10h fled on exposure with no control. Beth
   added inoculation: the warnings were discredited ("my ex-husband is starting rumours") before
   Sadie heard them. Still no C2, C3 or pursuit. **C4 alone does not move a case into the
   cycle.**
8. **The record laundered by settlement and resignation.** Natalia: earlier DV orders ended in
   settlements and dismissals, so the record looked thin. Claire/Riley and Arya: resignation in
   place of firing, so a later employer or licensing body sees nothing. Sage: police kept the
   targets apart. **These are separate institutions making the same move**; no coordination
   is implied (structural identity is not coordination).
9. **A predicted institutional misreading used as a threat (S13c).** "The cops think it's two
   girls fighting." The perpetrator's leverage was the institution's expected error, not his
   own power. Natalia's dilemma (a Black woman calling the state on a Black trans man; "red
   flags are red flags, no matter if they're rainbow colored") is recorded in her words.
   **Coding is by behaviour; his identity is not a field.**
10. **Protective intervention that repeats the control (S13i).** Birdie's parents locked her in a
    car to force a rape kit; her mother called a lawyer and told others against her wishes.
    That is not coded as perpetration. It is a **both-victims-lens datum for doc 04**:
    victim-protective procedure has to include the victim's veto ("let me deal with it the way
    that I want to deal with it").
11. **Perpetrator change, observed (S13a).** Years after the arson, after a sentence, a
    programme and stepped supervision, Jake "shows up" consistently as a co-parent. Recorded
    as an outcome, **not as variance in the earlier behaviour.** For doc 06 it is one datum:
    change after imposed consequence and structure. Whether he reached it himself is
    undetermined. Amelia: "take him for who he is in front of me in this moment."
12. **Confound recorded (S13a).** Amphetamine use during the acute exit phase. The devaluation
    and economic pattern preceded it, so the confound qualifies the intensity of the R-SEP
    phase, not the presence of the cycle.
13. **Divine sanction as a veto (S13o, S13b).** "The date we prayed about… God gave us the
    answer." Odessa's "duty as a pastor's wife." This joins S5a ("God told me… and he confirmed
    it") as a device: **religious authority placed above the target's objection.** As in S5
    (Rachel): "it wasn't the belief system."
