## Divergences

1. **The institution's script pre-discredits the child to the parent**, six times. This is
   C4-T1 run by an organisation, and it is the move the power-vector test coded least often
   (pathologizing absent from the agreed segments). S24 is where it lives; cite it as a case.
2. **Peer enforcement makes victims into enforcers**, who carry moral injury (E18, E20) and
   one of whom re-ran an exercise on a roommate (E16). The codebook codes the conduct; the
   enforcers are also targets.
3. **The §2 echo (S24b).** The person who could leave or expose the family is confined on a
   parent's account and called the problem (E16 after a family intervention against a violent
   home; E20 after leaving a church; E04's "bipolar at eight"; E10's 72-hour hold as
   discipline). Keating names the lineage: status-offence incarceration on a parent's word.
4. **The comparator the season supplies.** Rules the child chose (Max at a transitional
   facility), independent evaluation (E04, E22), and the one court that refused a placement.
   Small n and the narrators' attribution; consistent with doc 06's self-directed constraint.
5. **Repair, flat and not.** Three parents apologised without a remainder ("If I had known
   this, I would never have done this… What can I do?"). Others offered the care rationale
   as the apology ("I was trying to save your life" — "That's not an apology").
6. **A fair complaint against the show.** The founder's attorneys objected to the short
   deadline; the show gave little notice. Recorded as fair on its face.
