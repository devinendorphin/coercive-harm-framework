# Research: Coerced-Suicide / "Psychological Homicide" Prosecutions

*Compiled 2026-07-09 for `/develop 03`. Directly tests the seed's psychological-
homicide theory (L26–L34).*

## Bottom line up front

- US law has convicted for a suicide caused by another's words — but only via
  **involuntary manslaughter**, in the narrow fact pattern of **active, contemporaneous
  encouragement of an imminent, specific suicide**, not the seed's model of a
  **slow-burn erosion over months/years**. [ESTABLISHED for the narrow pattern;
  the slow-burn extension is HYPOTHESIS/NORMATIVE]
- The **First Amendment "pure speech" defense was raised and rejected** at the state
  high-court level, and the US Supreme Court **denied cert** — so "it's just words"
  is not, by itself, a shield. But the holding is fact-bound. [ESTABLISHED]

## Commonwealth v. Carter (Mass. 2019), cert denied 2020

- Facts: Michelle Carter, via text and a phone call, actively urged Conrad Roy (18)
  to complete a suicide already in progress (told him to get back in the
  carbon-monoxide-filled truck). Roy died July 12, 2014.
- Charge/verdict: **involuntary manslaughter** (bench trial), affirmed by the
  Massachusetts SJC (481 Mass. 352 / 115 N.E.3d 559, 2019). Sentence 2.5 yrs, 15
  months to serve.
- **First Amendment:** SJC held her claim "lack[ed] merit" — "cannot escape liability
  just because she happened to use words to carry out her illegal act." Speech
  integral to criminal conduct is unprotected. **US Supreme Court denied certiorari**
  (Carter v. Massachusetts, 140 S. Ct. 910 (2020)). [ESTABLISHED]
- The Harvard Law Review case comment (vol. 131) and multiple law-review pieces flag
  the ruling as doctrinally unsettled / "blurring" the speech-conduct line — i.e.,
  the win is real but contested and narrow. [SUPPORTED]

## Commonwealth v. You (Mass., charged 2019)

- Inyoung You charged with involuntary manslaughter over boyfriend Alexander
  Urtula's suicide (leapt from a garage, May 2019). ~75,000 texts in two months,
  ~47,000 from You; prosecution theory: "complete and total control" + a "pattern of
  abusive speech." She raised a First Amendment / criminalizing-speech defense.
- **Significance:** closer to the seed's model than Carter — it alleges a *pattern of
  coercive control culminating in suicide*, not a single episode of encouragement.

## Implications for doc 03

- The **"psychological/coercive homicide"** mechanism has a real doctrinal toehold,
  but the enacted reality is **involuntary manslaughter for active encouragement of
  an imminent act** — a much smaller thing than "abuse over years drove her to drink
  herself to death." The gap between the two is exactly where the seed's theory is
  NORMATIVE, not SUPPORTED.
- **Mens rea problem (ties to doc 02):** Carter turned on a finding that she *wanted*
  Roy dead and acted to cause it. If the cluster conduct is genuinely
  reactive/improvisational (doc 02) rather than intentional, the specific-intent path
  Carter used may not be available; a recklessness/wanton-conduct theory is the more
  plausible fit, and even that needs a *specific, provable* act-to-death causal chain
  that slow-burn cases characteristically lack.
- **Causation is the wall.** Manslaughter needs but-for + proximate causation to a
  death. A years-long erosion with many intervening causes (addiction, other
  stressors, the victim's own agency) is far harder to pin to the accused than a
  real-time "get back in the truck." Doc 03 should treat slow-burn homicide as an
  **aspiration requiring doctrinal invention**, and flag it to `/steelman` and
  `/liberty-review` rather than presenting it as chargeable today.
