## Source quality, before anything else

The claims rest on named peer-reviewed and statutory sources, and those sources are mostly the right ones.
**Many links, though, point to secondary pages**: grokipedia, Wikipedia, Substack, Quora, CliffsNotes, Medium,
clinic marketing pages (valleyspringrecovery, cerevity) and advocacy summaries (Women's Aid). Those pages
relay figures; they are not the figures' source. Before a number enters a doc as `[ESTABLISHED]`, read it in
the primary (ONS, Scottish Government, COPFS, the journal article). Specific items to check when used:
- E&W 2024 court figures (1,299 proceeded against; 853 convicted; 832 male): the packet has them via
  Women's Aid; get the MoJ table.
- Hawaii's extension "through June 2031": the source is a *Psychology Today* blog dated September 2026.
- Brennan, Myhill et al. (2021), the 41 % arrest increase: no link is given.
- The packet says the Smith & Freyd (2013) table supports two of the four interactions the text claims.
  That is a sharp claim about a primary source; check the table before repeating it.
- NPD 6.2 % vs the 1.06 % mean (Dhawan 2010) and the Trull (2010) reanalysis: check before use.

## On Endorphin's prevalence riff (2026-11-12: "1 to 6 %… gravitate towards positions of great influence")

- **The range holds, with the instrument named.** NPD runs from a 1.06 % community mean to a 6.2 %
  NESARC upper bound; psychopathy is 1.2 % by PCL and 4.5 % pooled across instruments. "Single-digit,
  roughly 1–6 %, instrument-dependent" is `[SUPPORTED]`.
- **"Gravitate towards influence" is `[SUPPORTED]`, thin.** The organisational psychopathy figure, 12.9 %,
  comes from three samples (n = 668) using mostly self-report. Babiak et al. found 3.9 % in a single
  convenience sample of managers.
- **Boundary (S-1, S-16, agreement 3).** Trait prevalence is background only. The framework codes conduct.
  The NESARC demographic correlates (by race, ethnicity, marital status) are **never** imported into
  anything this project publishes or proposes.

## What it weakens or leaves open for us

1. The series anchor "same moves" is supported as a **recognisable cluster**, not as a closed or universal
   list (Johnson's typology; no exhaustiveness test). That matches our own scope cases and the audit.
2. "Every room" (arc 2) needs the qualifier "parallel literatures and cases, not one measured instrument".
3. Whether criminalisation reduces harm is unknown. Doc 03 must not imply it does.
