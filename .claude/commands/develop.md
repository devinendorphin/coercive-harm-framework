Target: $ARGUMENTS (a doc number/name, e.g. "03" or "legal-framework". If empty,
consult sessions/LATEST.md for the highest-priority open workstream and confirm with
Endorphin before proceeding.)

Loop for this session:

1. **Orient.** Read the target doc, its "Open questions," and sessions/LATEST.md.
   State in 3–5 sentences where this workstream currently stands and the single most
   valuable thing to advance today. Get Endorphin's confirmation or redirection.

2. **Research first, draft second.** If the section rests on claims tagged
   [HYPOTHESIS] or [SUPPORTED], do targeted web research before drafting: current
   statutes (coercive control laws in England/Wales, Scotland, Ireland, California,
   Hawaii, Connecticut, etc.), relevant case law (e.g., abuse-linked suicide
   prosecutions), peer-reviewed literature (interpersonal neurobiology, IFS efficacy,
   mindfulness and mPFC structural change). Write findings to `research/` — one file
   per topic, with full citations — before touching the doc.

3. **Draft in dialogue.** Propose concrete text for the doc in small pieces (a
   subsection at a time), thinking out loud about tradeoffs. Endorphin's edits and
   spoken riffs are primary source material — integrate them, don't just append them.

4. **Tag as you go.** Every new claim gets an epistemic-status tag. Every new legal
   mechanism gets a `NEEDS-LIBERTY-REVIEW` marker until /liberty-review clears it.

5. **Close.** Update the doc's "Open questions," note anything that belongs in
   docs/08-objections, and remind Endorphin to run /session-log.

Honesty constraint (per CLAUDE.md working agreement 1): at least once per session,
identify the weakest load-bearing element in the current draft and say so plainly.
