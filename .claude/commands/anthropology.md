Target: $ARGUMENTS (a specific term/culture/tradition, an axis from the Part B
taxonomy, or a Part C research thread. If empty, pick the highest-value unverified
entry in docs/11 and confirm with Endorphin.)

Protocol for each research target:

1. **Primary-source discipline.** Prefer ethnographies, translated primary texts,
   legal-historical scholarship, and peer-reviewed anthropology over listicles and
   pop-psychology retellings. The kunlangeta in particular is widely misquoted
   online — always anchor to Murphy (1976) and any subsequent scholarly commentary,
   including critiques of Murphy's framing. For religious-legal traditions (herem,
   meidung, munafiq, icchantika), find scholarship by specialists inside or close
   to the tradition, not only outside observers.

2. **Fill the full entry schema** (docs/11 Part A format): term, culture, documented
   definition, associated intervention, and — with high detail per Endorphin's
   request — the intervention's trigger, reversibility, theory of change, contrition
   test, abuse safeguards, scale, and documented failure modes (Part B axes).
   Write the detailed findings to `research/anthro-<term>.md` with full citations;
   update the docs/11 entry with a summary and pointer.

3. **Colonial/exoticization check.** Much of this literature was recorded by
   outsiders. Note who documented each term, when, under what power relations, and
   whether members of the culture contest the standard account (the windigo
   literature is the type case). Do not flatten living traditions into framework
   fodder — record internal debates.

4. **Witch-trial audit.** For every naming/intervention system researched, explicitly
   answer: is there historical evidence this system was weaponized against innocents?
   What calibrated it or failed to? Feed findings to docs/07 and the docs/11 Open
   Questions.

5. **Bridge to the framework.** End each session with 2–5 concrete integration memos:
   "Tradition X's mechanism Y suggests amendment Z to workstream N." Examples of the
   expected grain: meidung's staged-reintegration protocol → a contrition-testing
   procedure for workstream 03's rehabilitative track; xiaoren role-restriction →
   precedent language for workstream 05's no-financial-authority accommodation
   template; ostracism's faction-capture failure → a required safeguard in
   workstream 07. Endorphin accepts or rejects each memo; accepted ones get
   implemented in the target doc with a citation back to research/.

6. **Implementation-research pass.** Where a modern analog exists (restorative
   justice, peacemaking courts, MJTC-style intensive treatment, shunning studies),
   search for outcome evaluations and record effect sizes, populations, and
   limitations honestly — including null and negative results. The framework is
   strengthened, not weakened, by knowing what failed.
